# Buy BC Partnership Program - Eligibility Checklist (2026 Cycle)

## CRITICAL: Pre-Application Requirements

### ✅ Buy BC Logo Licence - MANDATORY
**You MUST have a Buy BC logo licence BEFORE applying**

- [ ] Logo licence obtained from Buy BC program
- [ ] Logo licence is valid through November 13, 2026 (end of project period)
- [ ] Logo licence number documented and ready to provide
- **Deadline to obtain licence:** February 13, 2026
- **Where to apply:** buybc.com

**⚠️ WITHOUT A VALID LOGO LICENCE, YOUR APPLICATION WILL BE INELIGIBLE**

## Applicant Eligibility

### Stream 1: Producers, Processors, and Cooperatives

#### Organization Type
- [ ] Primary producer of BC agriculture/food/seafood products
- [ ] Processor of BC agriculture/food/seafood products
- [ ] Agricultural cooperative operating in BC

#### Location Requirements
- [ ] Organization is based in British Columbia
- [ ] Primary operations are in BC
- [ ] Registered/incorporated in BC (or Canada with BC operations)

#### Business Requirements
- [ ] Active business operations
- [ ] Produces or processes agriculture, food, seafood, or beverage products
- [ ] Products are eligible for Buy BC logo licence
- [ ] In good standing with BC Registry (if incorporated)

#### Revenue Requirements
- [ ] Minimum $100,000 annual revenue from agriculture/food products in BOTH 2024 AND 2025
- [ ] Can provide financial documentation if requested
- [ ] Revenue is from BC agriculture/food/seafood/beverage products

**Funding Tier Based on Revenue:**
- If BOTH 2024 and 2025 revenue < $250,000: **Maximum $15,000**
- If EITHER 2024 or 2025 revenue ≥ $250,000: **Maximum $30,000**

## Eligible Activity Types (Must Select At Least One)

### 1. Paid Advertising ✅
**Eligible:**
- Digital advertising (Google Ads, social media, website banners)
- Print advertising (magazines, newspapers)
- Radio advertising
- Television advertising
- Out-of-home advertising (billboards, transit ads)
- Influencer marketing (up to $5,000 maximum)

**Ineligible:**
- Advertising outside BC
- Advertising without Buy BC logo
- General brand advertising not specific to project

**Funding Cap:** No maximum (except $5,000 for influencer marketing)

### 2. In-Store Demonstrations and Promotions ✅
**Eligible:**
- Product demonstrations in BC retail stores
- Sampling programs in stores/restaurants
- Brand ambassador programs
- In-store promotional displays and signage
- Demo staff wages (within staff wage limits)

**Ineligible:**
- Demos outside BC
- Events without Buy BC logo prominently displayed
- Permanent store fixtures

**Funding Cap:** No maximum

### 3. Labelling Products or Packaging with Buy BC Logo ✅
**Eligible:**
- Design of labels/packaging incorporating Buy BC logo
- Printing of labels/packaging with Buy BC logo
- Package prototyping with logo
- Label application equipment costs

**Ineligible:**
- Labels/packaging without Buy BC logo
- Labelling for export markets
- General rebranding not specific to Buy BC

**Funding Cap:** $10,000 per applicant per year

### 4. B.C. Trade Shows and Sales Expositions ✅
**Eligible:**
- Trade show booth rental fees
- Booth design and construction/rental
- Graphics, signage, and displays featuring Buy BC logo
- Product samples for distribution
- Shipping costs for samples/materials
- Event-specific marketing (programs, banners)
- Chef services for food preparation
- Kitchen equipment rental
- Storage rental
- Booth services (electricity, garbage, water)
- Translators for non-English events

**Ineligible:**
- Trade shows outside BC
- General travel and accommodation expenses
- Events not focused on BC market
- Booth materials without Buy BC logo

**Funding Cap:** No maximum

### 5. Marketing Collateral and Promotional Materials ✅
**Eligible:**
- Brochures, flyers, and rack cards with Buy BC logo
- Posters and banners with logo
- Point-of-sale materials with logo
- Shelf-talkers and menu inserts with logo
- Recipe cards with logo
- Videos and photography with logo
- Product catalogues with logo
- Branded apparel worn by staff (up to $500 maximum)
- Pull-up banners with logo
- Coasters and promotional items with logo

**Ineligible:**
- Materials without Buy BC logo
- Materials for export markets
- General business signage unrelated to promotion
- Excessive apparel purchases (over $500)

**Funding Cap:** $5,000 per applicant per year ($500 maximum for apparel)

### 6. Staff Wages ✅
**Eligible:**
- Wages for staff directly implementing project activities
- Marketing coordinator time for Buy BC activities
- Demo staff conducting in-store promotions
- Administrative support for project delivery
- Clearly defined tasks connected to funded activities

**Ineligible:**
- General operations or overhead
- Staff time not directly related to project
- Executive salaries unconnected to project
- Wages without clear project connection

**Funding Cap:**
- **Producers/Processors:** $2,000 per year maximum
- **Industry Associations:** $10,000 per year maximum

- Regular business overhead costs
- Utilities and rent
- General administrative expenses
- Insurance costs
- Business development not specific to project

### ❌ Capital Purchases
- Equipment purchases (except short-term rental)
- Vehicles
- Permanent fixtures or infrastructure
- Technology purchases (computers, tablets)
- Real estate or building improvements

### ❌ Non-BC Market Activities
- Export market development
- Marketing in other Canadian provinces
- International trade activities
- Distribution outside BC

### ❌ Activities Without Buy BC Logo
- Any marketing not featuring Buy BC logo prominently
- Generic product promotion
- Branding that doesn't incorporate Buy BC identity

### ❌ Ineligible Costs
- Cost of obtaining Buy BC logo licence
- HST/GST (except where non-recoverable)
- Interest and bank charges
- Entertainment and alcohol
- Legal fees
- Audit costs
- Fundraising expenses
- Costs incurred before April 1, 2026 or after November 13, 2026

### ❌ Previous Commitments
- Expenses already paid or committed before April 1, 2026
- Retroactive funding for past activities
- Invoices dated before project start date

## Financial Eligibility

### Cost-Share Requirements

**Stream 1 (Producers/Processors/Cooperatives):**
- **First-time applicants:** 50% Ministry / 50% Applicant
  - Example: $20,000 project = $10,000 Ministry + $10,000 Applicant
- **Returning recipients (2023+):** 35% Ministry / 65% Applicant
  - Example: $20,000 project = $7,000 Ministry + $13,000 Applicant

**Stream 2 (Industry Associations):**
- **All applicants:** 70% Ministry / 30% Applicant
  - Example: $50,000 project = $35,000 Ministry + $15,000 Applicant

### Cash Match Requirements
- [ ] Can provide required cash match percentage
- [ ] Cash match is from organization's own funds (not other grants)
- [ ] Cash match is for eligible project expenses only
- [ ] Can demonstrate financial capacity to complete project

### Funding Limits
- [ ] Total Ministry funding request does not exceed maximum for your category
- [ ] Activity-specific caps are not exceeded
- [ ] Budget is realistic and achievable

## Cannabis Products - Special Eligibility Rules

### ✅ Cannabis Products ARE Eligible for Buy BC Funding

**HOWEVER, must comply with all Cannabis Control and Licensing Act requirements:**

- [ ] Cannot market to persons under 19 years of age
- [ ] Cannot include health claims about cannabis products
- [ ] Cannot include cosmetic claims about cannabis products
- [ ] Cannot promote over-consumption
- [ ] Marketing must comply with provincial health warning requirements
- [ ] Logo placement must meet Cannabis Act restrictions
- [ ] All advertising must comply with BC cannabis marketing regulations

**Eligible Activities:**
- Packaging with Buy BC logo (within health warning requirements)
- Trade show participation (adult-only events)
- Collateral materials complying with regulations
- Advertising in permitted channels only

**Ineligible:**
- Any marketing targeting youth
- Health or cosmetic claims
- Promotion of over-consumption
- Non-compliant advertising channels

## Common Eligibility Questions

**Q: Can I apply if I'm a new business with less than 2 years of revenue?**
A: Stream 1 requires $100K revenue in BOTH 2024 and 2025. New businesses may not qualify unless they can demonstrate this revenue. Stream 2 (associations) may have more flexibility.

**Q: Can I include activities in Alberta or other provinces?**
A: No. All Buy BC activities must target the BC market only. Export or interprovincial activities are not eligible.

**Q: What if I don't have my logo licence yet?**
A: You must obtain your logo licence by February 13, 2026. Apply for it immediately at buybc.com. You cannot submit your application without it.

**Q: Can I apply for the same project I did last year?**
A: You can apply for similar activities, but it should show growth, expansion, or new markets. Exact repeats may score lower. Note: If you received funding in 2023+, your cost-share is reduced to 35/65.

**Q: Are online sales platforms eligible?**
A: Yes, if they sell BC products to BC consumers and prominently feature Buy BC logo. E-commerce activities targeting BC market are eligible.

**Q: Can I hire contractors instead of employees?**
A: Yes, contractor fees for eligible services (design, marketing, demo staff, etc.) can be included in appropriate activity categories. Staff wages category is specifically for employee wages.

**Q: What if my project needs to extend past November 13, 2026?**
A: All project activities and expenses must be completed by November 13, 2026. No extensions. Plan your timeline accordingly.

**Q: Can associations apply on behalf of their members?**
A: Yes, associations can apply for projects that benefit their members. This is Stream 2 with different funding limits and cost-share ratio.
