# Buy BC Partnership Program - Application Questions Guide (2026 Cycle)

## Overview
This document contains all application questions and fields from the MNP Application Portal for the Buy BC Partnership Program 2026 cycle. Use this to guide applicants through every section of the application.

---

## Section 8: Project Objectives & Alignment (Merit Criterion 1 - 25%)

### 8.1 Project Goals
**Describe the specific goals and objectives of your project (max 1000 words)**

Address:
- Primary objectives (what you aim to achieve)
- Alignment with Buy BC program goals (promote BC products in BC market)
- Target market within BC (retailers, consumers, food service, etc.)
- How project promotes local agriculture/food products
- Innovation in marketing approach

**Strong responses include:**
- Specific, measurable objectives (e.g., "Increase retail distribution by 25%")
- Clear target markets within BC
- Alignment with Buy BC mission
- Unique or innovative approaches

---

## Section 9: Project Work Plan (Merit Criterion 2 - 40% - HIGHEST WEIGHT)

### 9.1 Detailed Work Plan
**Provide a comprehensive work plan with timeline (max 2000 words)**

Must include:
- **Activities:** Specific tasks to be completed
- **Timeline:** Month-by-month schedule (April 2026 - November 2026)
- **Milestones:** Key deliverables and completion dates
- **Budget justification:** How each expense supports objectives
- **Feasibility:** Realistic scope within project period

**Activity Timeline Table Format:**
| Activity | Description | Start Date | End Date | Budget Allocation |
|----------|-------------|------------|----------|-------------------|
| Activity 1 | Detail | MM/YYYY | MM/YYYY | $X,XXX |
| Activity 2 | Detail | MM/YYYY | MM/YYYY | $X,XXX |

**Strong responses include:**
- Detailed, realistic timeline
- Clear connection between activities and objectives
- Comprehensive budget justification
- Feasible scope
- Risk mitigation strategies

---

## Section 10: Expected Project Impact (Merit Criterion 3 - 25%)

### 10.1 Outcomes and KPIs
**Describe the expected outcomes and impact of your project (max 1000 words)**

Must include:
- **Quantifiable outcomes** (sales increase, new retail partnerships, consumer reach)
- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** with baseline and targets
- **Long-term sustainability** beyond project period
- **Economic impact** for BC agriculture/food sector
- **Market penetration** and brand awareness goals

### 10.2 Key Performance Indicators

**Provide specific KPIs for your project:**

Example KPIs by activity type:
- **Paid Advertising:** Views/reach, impressions, clicks, website traffic increase
- **In-Store Demos:** Consumers engaged, demos conducted, product samples distributed, sales increase
- **Labelling/Packaging:** New products with Buy BC logo, number of labels/packages produced
- **Tradeshows:** New retail partnerships, product listings, trade leads, sales agreements
- **Marketing Collateral:** Materials produced, materials distributed, consumer engagement

**For each KPI provide:**
- Baseline (current state)
- Target (goal by project end)
- Measurement method

## Section 13: Activity Selection & Details

### 13.1 Select Activities
**Check all eligible activities your project will include:**

- [ ] Paid Advertising
- [ ] In-Store Demonstrations and Promotions
- [ ] Labelling Products or Packaging with Buy BC Logo
- [ ] B.C. Trade Shows and Sales Expositions
- [ ] Marketing Collateral and Promotional Materials
- [ ] Staff Wages

### 13.2 Activity-Specific Questions

**For EACH selected activity, provide:**

#### Paid Advertising
- Types of advertising (digital, print, radio, TV, transit, influencer)
- Target audience and geographic reach within BC
- Campaign duration and frequency
- Expected impressions/reach/engagement
- How Buy BC logo will be featured
- KPIs (views, clicks, impressions, website traffic increase)

**Funding Cap:** $5,000 maximum for influencer marketing

#### In-Store Demonstrations and Promotions
- Number of demos/promotions planned
- Retail locations (list stores/chains)
- Products to be promoted
- Expected consumer engagement
- How Buy BC logo will be featured
- KPIs (consumers engaged, samples distributed, sales increase)

**Funding Cap:** No maximum

#### Labelling Products or Packaging with Buy BC Logo
- Number of products to receive Buy BC logo
- New labels vs. repackaging existing products
- Design approach and supplier
- Distribution plan
- Expected production volume
- KPIs (new products with logo, units produced)

**Funding Cap:** $10,000 maximum per applicant per year

#### B.C. Trade Shows and Sales Expositions
- Name and date of trade show(s)
- Target buyers (retail, food service, distributors)
- Booth activities and product sampling
- Expected trade leads and partnerships
- How Buy BC logo will be featured
- KPIs (new partnerships, product listings, sales agreements)

**Funding Cap:** No maximum

#### Marketing Collateral and Promotional Materials
- Types of materials (brochures, posters, banners, videos, POS, etc.)
- Quantity and distribution plan
- How Buy BC logo will be featured
- Target audience and locations
- Expected consumer engagement
- KPIs (materials produced, materials distributed)

**Funding Cap:** $5,000 maximum per applicant per year ($500 maximum for branded apparel)

#### Staff Wages
- Staff position(s) and role in project
- Percentage of time dedicated to Buy BC activities
- Specific tasks to be funded
- How position supports project objectives
- Clear connection to other funded activities

**Funding Cap:**
- Producers/Processors: $2,000 maximum
- Industry Associations: $10,000 maximum

## Common Application Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No Buy BC Logo Licence:** Must have licence BEFORE applying
2. **Incorrect cost-share ratio:** Returning recipients get 35/65, not 50/50
3. **Exceeding activity caps:** $10K labelling, $5K collateral, $5K influencers
4. **Vague objectives:** Must be specific and measurable
5. **Weak work plan:** Timeline and budget must be detailed and realistic
6. **Missing KPIs:** Must provide baseline, target, and measurement method
7. **Non-BC activities:** All activities must target BC market only
8. **Missing quotes:** Required for all items ≥ $5,000
9. **Logo not featured:** Buy BC logo must be prominently featured in all activities
10. **Unrealistic timeline:** Must complete project by November 13, 2026

## Merit Scoring Optimization Tips

**Project Work Plan (40% - Focus Here!):**
- Provide month-by-month timeline with specific dates
- Include detailed budget justification for every line item
- Show clear connection between activities and objectives
- Demonstrate cost-effectiveness
- Include contingency planning

**Project Objectives & Alignment (25%):**
- Align explicitly with Buy BC program goals
- Target specific BC markets with data
- Show innovation in approach
- Quantify expected outcomes

**Expected Project Impact (25%):**
- Provide robust KPIs with baselines and targets
- Show long-term sustainability
- Demonstrate economic impact for BC
- Include market data supporting projections

**Past Performance (10%):**
- If returning recipient, emphasize strong track record
- Highlight on-time reporting and objective achievement
- Show financial accountability
