## Merit Evaluation Criteria (How Your Application is Scored)

**Total Score: 100 points**

Applications are NOT asked separate merit questions. Instead, all responses throughout the application are evaluated using these four criteria:

### Criterion 1: Project Objectives & Alignment with Program Outcomes (25%)

**What evaluators look for:**
- Projects that demonstrate alignment with 5 or 6 of the Immediate Outcomes identified in Appendix 1 will be scored more favourably
- Clear alignment with Buy BC program goals (promote BC products in BC market)
- Specific, measurable objectives
- Well-defined target market within BC
- Innovation in marketing approach

**Where this is assessed:**
- Section 4: Project Objectives and Description
- Section 5: Project Activities descriptions
- Overall application coherence

---

### Criterion 2: Project Work Plan (40%) ⭐ **HIGHEST WEIGHT**

**What evaluators look for:**
- Project activities are comprehensive, clear and well defined (i.e., reasonable timelines, service providers, deliverables, etc.)
- Budget and timeline are reasonable, the size of the project/budget is realistic based on the size/capacity of the business, supported by quotes for amounts over $5,000, and includes only eligible costs

**Where this is assessed:**
- Section 5: Project Activities - timeline and implementation details for each activity
- Section 6: Project Budget - comprehensive, realistic budget with quotes
- Feasibility of completing all activities within April 1 - November 13, 2026

**Strong applications include:**
- Month-by-month timeline with specific dates
- Service providers named for each activity
- Detailed budget justification
- Quotes for all items ≥$5,000
- Realistic scope for organization size
- Clear connection between budget and activities

---

### Criterion 3: Expected Project Impact (25%)

**What evaluators look for:**
- Applicant has clearly demonstrated how impact will be measured for each activity at the end of the project, including clearly defined targets that are realistic to the budget, timeline, and type of activity supported
- Project/Applicant aligns with one or more Program Priorities

**Where this is assessed:**
- Section 5: Project Activities - KPI questions for each activity
- Targets and expected results fields
- Alignment with diversity priorities (Section 1)

**Strong applications include:**
- Specific, measurable KPIs for each activity
- Baseline → Target → Measurement method
- Realistic projections based on budget and timeline
- Long-term sustainability beyond project period
- Alignment with program priorities (Indigenous, women, youth ownership)

---

### Criterion 4: Past Project Reporting and Performance (10%)

**What evaluators look for:**
- **First-time applicants:** Applicants that have not received funding through the Buy BC Partnership Program previously will be awarded the full 10% for this criteria
- **Previous recipients:** Will have their future applications assessed against:
  - Past projects have been within budget with timely reporting
  - Applicant has been responsive and professional in their previous communications with Program Administration
  - Past projects have met or surpassed targeted results

**Where this is assessed:**
- Section 1: Previous Funding History question
- Administrative records from past Buy BC projects (if applicable)

## Common Application Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No Buy BC Logo Licence**
   - MUST have licence BEFORE applying
   - Deadline: February 13, 2026

2. **Incorrect Cost-Share Ratio**
   - Returning recipients (2023+) get 35/65, not 50/50
   - Check funding history question carefully

3. **Exceeding Activity Caps**
   - $10K labelling maximum
   - $5K marketing collateral maximum
   - $5K influencer marketing maximum
   - $2K/$10K staff wages maximum

4. **Vague Project Descriptions**
   - Be specific about what, where, when, how
   - Name service providers, locations, retailers
   - Provide exact timeline

5. **Weak Work Plan (40% of score!)**
   - Don't just list activities - provide detailed timeline
   - Must include service providers for each activity
   - Budget must be fully justified
   - Missing quotes for items ≥$5,000 hurts this score

6. **Missing or Weak KPIs**
   - Must provide KPIs for EACH activity
   - Must include baseline, target, measurement method
   - "Increase sales" is not specific enough
   - "Increase sales by 25% from 2,000 to 2,500 units measured through retailer POS data" is good

7. **Non-BC Activities**
   - All activities MUST target BC market only
   - Export activities are NOT eligible
   - Marketing outside BC is NOT eligible

8. **Buy BC Logo Not Prominent**
   - Logo must be featured prominently in ALL activities
   - Describe how logo will appear in each activity
   - Cannot use other branding more prominently than Buy BC logo

9. **Unrealistic Timeline**
   - Project period: April 1, 2026 - November 13, 2026 (7.5 months)
   - All activities must complete by November 13
   - Account for design, production, and execution time

10. **Budget Doesn't Match Activities**
    - Every budget line item must relate to described activities
    - Every described activity must appear in budget
    - Amounts must be realistic and justified

## Tips for Maximizing Your Score

### Focus on Project Work Plan (40% of Score!)

This is the HIGHEST WEIGHTED criterion. Invest significant effort here.

**Strong Work Plans Include:**
- **Detailed Timeline:** Month-by-month from April to November 2026
  - Example: "April 2026: Finalize booth design (Week 1-2), Order marketing collateral (Week 2), Confirm demo locations (Week 3-4)"
- **Specific Service Providers:** Name actual companies
  - Example: "XYZ Design Studio (Vancouver) will create booth graphics" not just "graphic designer"
- **Clear Deliverables:** What will be produced
  - Example: "5,000 brochures, 20 banners, 1 promotional video"
- **Budget Justification:** Why each expense is necessary
  - Example: "$3,500 for 10 in-store demos @ $350 each. Professional demo staff will conduct 4-hour sampling events at major Metro Vancouver grocery stores, distributing 200 samples per event, expected to generate 2,000+ consumer touchpoints"
- **Quotes:** Actual quotes for items ≥$5,000
- **Feasibility:** Realistic scope for your organization size

### Strengthen Project Objectives & Alignment (25%)

**Clear Objectives:**
- Specific and measurable: "Increase retail distribution from 15 to 25 stores (67% increase) in Metro Vancouver"
- Not vague: "Raise awareness and increase sales"

**Demonstrate Alignment:**
- Explicitly reference Buy BC program goals
- Show how project promotes BC products in BC market
- Connect to Appendix 1 Immediate Outcomes

**Target Market:**
- Be specific: "Metro Vancouver grocery stores (Save-On-Foods, Thrifty Foods, Urban Fare chains)"
- Not vague: "BC consumers"

**Innovation:**
- Explain what's unique or effective about your approach
- Show why this will work for your products

### Demonstrate Expected Impact (25%)

**Robust KPIs for Each Activity:**

Format: Baseline → Target → Measurement

**Paid Advertising Example:**
- Views: "Achieve 150,000 impressions across digital and print channels (baseline: 0, target: 150,000, measure: ad platform analytics)"
- Website traffic: "Increase monthly visitors by 100% from 800 to 1,600 (Google Analytics)"

**In-Store Demos Example:**
- Consumer engagement: "Engage 2,000+ consumers across 10 demo events (baseline: 0, target: 2,000, measure: demo staff count sheets)"
- Sales lift: "15% sales increase during demo periods measured through retailer POS data"

**Tradeshows Example:**
- New partnerships: "Secure 5 new retail partnership agreements (baseline: 15 stores, target: 20 stores, measure: signed agreements)"
- Sales value: "$75,000 in new annual sales agreements (baseline: $X, target: $X + $75K, measure: purchase orders)"

**Show Long-Term Impact:**
- "Partnerships established will continue beyond project period"
- "Marketing materials usable for 2+ years"
- "Projected sustained annual sales increase of $100K supporting 2 FTE jobs"

### Address Past Performance (10%)

**If First-Time Applicant:**
- Automatic full 10% - no action needed

**If Returning Recipient:**
- Proactively describe past achievements
- Highlight on-time reporting
- Show results exceeded targets
- Demonstrate professional relationship with program administration
