# Buy BC Partnership Program - Activity Examples & Scenarios (2026 Cycle)

## Overview
This document provides real-world examples of eligible and ineligible activities, budget scenarios, and common questions to help applicants understand what can and cannot be funded under the Buy BC Partnership Program.

---

## Activity Type 1: Paid Advertising

### ✅ ELIGIBLE Examples

**Example 1: Social Media Advertising Campaign**
- **Activity:** 3-month Instagram and Facebook ad campaign targeting Metro Vancouver consumers
- **Budget:** $4,000 (ad spend) + $1,000 (creative design) = $5,000
- **Why Eligible:** Digital advertising promoting BC products to BC consumers, Buy BC logo featured in all ads
- **KPIs:** 100,000 impressions, 2,500 clicks, 2.5% CTR, 50% increase in website traffic

**Example 2: Transit Advertising**
- **Activity:** Bus shelter ads in 20 locations across Victoria
- **Budget:** $8,000 (4-week campaign)
- **Why Eligible:** Out-of-home advertising in BC targeting local consumers with Buy BC logo
- **KPIs:** 500,000 impressions, increased brand awareness in target market
- **Note:** Requires quote (≥$5,000)

**Example 3: Influencer Marketing Campaign**
- **Activity:** Partner with 3 BC-based food bloggers for sponsored content
- **Budget:** $4,500 (within $5,000 influencer cap)
- **Why Eligible:** Influencer marketing to BC audience, Buy BC logo featured in content
- **KPIs:** 75,000 combined follower reach, 15 sponsored posts, 5% engagement rate

**Example 4: Print Advertising in BC Magazine**
- **Activity:** Half-page ads in BC Food & Beverage Magazine (3 issues)
- **Budget:** $2,400 (3 × $800)
- **Why Eligible:** Print advertising in BC publication targeting BC consumers
- **KPIs:** 25,000 circulation, 50,000 estimated readership

### ❌ INELIGIBLE Examples

**Example 1: Out-of-Province Advertising**
- **Activity:** Facebook ads targeting Alberta and Ontario consumers
- **Why Ineligible:** Buy BC program only funds BC market promotion
- **Alternative:** Focus ads on BC geographic targeting only

**Example 2: Excessive Influencer Spend**
- **Activity:** $8,000 for celebrity influencer partnership
- **Why Ineligible:** Exceeds $5,000 influencer marketing cap
- **Alternative:** Reduce to $5,000 or shift additional $3,000 to other advertising

**Example 3: Advertising Without Buy BC Logo**
- **Activity:** General brand advertising without Buy BC identity
- **Why Ineligible:** Buy BC logo must be prominently featured
- **Alternative:** Redesign ads to incorporate Buy BC logo

## Common Scenarios & Questions

### Scenario 1: Cannabis Producer
**Question:** Can a cannabis producer apply for Buy BC funding?

**Answer:** ✅ YES, cannabis products are eligible

**Requirements:**
- Must comply with Cannabis Control and Licensing Act
- Cannot market to persons under 19
- Cannot include health or cosmetic claims
- Cannot promote over-consumption
- Logo must comply with health warning requirements
- All advertising must follow provincial cannabis regulations

**Example Eligible Activity:**
- Compliant packaging with Buy BC logo
- Trade show booth at adult-only cannabis industry event
- Compliant marketing materials

---

### Scenario 2: First-Time vs. Returning Recipient
**Question:** How does returning recipient status affect my application?

**First-Time Applicant:**
- ✅ 50/50 cost-share ratio
- ✅ Automatically receives full 10 points for Past Performance
- ✅ Full funding potential

**Returning Recipient (2023+):**
- ⚠️ 35/65 cost-share ratio (significantly higher cash match required)
- 📊 Past performance evaluated (0-10 points based on track record)
- 💰 Same maximum funding, but higher co-investment

**Example:**
- $20,000 project
- First-time: $10,000 Ministry + $10,000 Match
- Returning: $7,000 Ministry + $13,000 Match (need $3,000 more cash)

---

### Scenario 3: Combining Multiple Activity Types
**Question:** Can I include activities from multiple categories?

**Answer:** ✅ YES, strongly encouraged for comprehensive strategy

**Best Practice:**
- Combine activities that support each other
- Example: Labels + Demos + Trade Show = integrated campaign
- Ensure each activity connects to objectives
- Stay within individual activity caps

---

### Scenario 4: Quotes for Budget Items
**Question:** Which expenses require quotes?

**Answer:** ALL items with total cost ≥ $5,000

**Examples Requiring Quotes:**
- $8,500 for labels (quote required)
- $6,000 trade show booth (quote required)
- $5,200 video production (quote required)

**Examples NOT Requiring Quotes:**
- $3,000 brochures (no quote needed)
- $4,500 advertising (no quote needed)
- $2,000 staff wages (wages don't require quotes)

---

### Scenario 5: Logo Licence Timing
**Question:** Can I apply for Buy BC funding before getting my logo licence?

**Answer:** ❌ NO - Logo licence is mandatory before applying

**Critical Deadlines:**
- Logo Licence Deadline: February 13, 2026
- Application Deadline: February 20, 2026

**Action:** Apply for logo licence immediately at buybc.com

---

### Scenario 6: Project Timeline Flexibility
**Question:** Can my project extend past November 13, 2026?

**Answer:** ❌ NO - All activities must complete by November 13, 2026

**Planning Tips:**
- Start activities early (April/May)
- Allow buffer time for delays
- Final report due shortly after project end
- No extensions available

## Final Checklist for Activity Selection

Before finalizing your activities:
- [ ] All activities target BC market only
- [ ] Buy BC logo featured prominently in all activities
- [ ] Activities occur within project period (April 1 - Nov 13, 2026)
- [ ] Activity-specific caps not exceeded
- [ ] Budget justified and realistic
- [ ] Quotes obtained for items ≥ $5,000
- [ ] Activities support stated objectives
- [ ] KPIs defined for each activity
- [ ] Total funding within your maximum eligible amount
- [ ] Cost-share ratio correctly applied (50/50, 35/65, or 70/30)
