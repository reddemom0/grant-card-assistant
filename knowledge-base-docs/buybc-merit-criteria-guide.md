# Buy BC Partnership Program - Merit Criteria Optimization Guide (2026 Cycle)

## Overview
Buy BC applications are evaluated using a merit-based scoring system. Understanding how applications are scored is critical to maximizing your chances of approval and funding.

**Total Score: 100 points**

---

## Merit Criteria Breakdown

### 1. Project Objectives & Alignment (25 points)
How well your project goals align with Buy BC program objectives

### 2. Project Work Plan (40 points) ⭐ **HIGHEST WEIGHT**
Quality, detail, and feasibility of your work plan and budget

### 3. Expected Project Impact (25 points)
Measurable outcomes and KPIs demonstrating project effectiveness

### 4. Past Reporting/Performance (10 points)
Previous Buy BC funding track record (returning recipients only)

---

## Criterion 1: Project Objectives & Alignment (25 points)

### What Reviewers Are Looking For:

**Clear Alignment with Buy BC Goals:**
- Promotes BC agriculture, food, seafood, or beverage products
- Targets BC consumers, retailers, food service, or distributors
- Increases awareness of local products
- Builds demand for BC products in BC market
- Strengthens BC food system

**Specific, Measurable Objectives:**
- Concrete goals, not vague aspirations
- Quantifiable targets (e.g., "Increase retail distribution by 25%")
- Realistic and achievable within project period
- Directly related to project activities

**Strategic Target Market:**
- Well-defined target audience within BC
- Understanding of market opportunity
- Clear strategy for reaching target market
- Evidence of market demand or research

**Innovation:**
- Unique or creative marketing approaches
- New channels or tactics
- Differentiation from competitors
- Value-added positioning

### High-Scoring Strategy:

**DO:**
✅ State 3-5 specific, measurable objectives
✅ Connect each objective directly to Buy BC program goals
✅ Define target market with demographic/geographic specificity
✅ Include market research or data supporting opportunity
✅ Explain why your approach is innovative or effective
✅ Show how objectives lead to sustainable growth

**Example High-Scoring Objective:**
"Increase retail distribution of our BC-grown organic vegetables in Metro Vancouver grocery stores from 15 to 25 locations (67% increase) by November 2026, measured through new retail partnership agreements. This directly supports Buy BC's goal of increasing availability of local produce in BC communities."

**DON'T:**
❌ Use vague language ("raise awareness," "increase sales" without numbers)
❌ Set unrealistic goals given timeline and resources
❌ Focus solely on your business benefit (emphasize BC sector benefit)
❌ Ignore market research or evidence of demand
❌ Copy generic marketing objectives from templates

### Common Mistakes:
- Too broad or vague objectives
- No connection to Buy BC program goals
- Lack of market understanding
- No differentiation or innovation
- Objectives don't match activities

### Word Count: Maximum 1000 words
**Recommended:** 800-950 words for comprehensive coverage

## Criterion 2: Project Work Plan (40 points) ⭐ **FOCUS HERE**

### What Reviewers Are Looking For:

**Detailed Timeline:**
- Month-by-month breakdown of activities
- Specific start and end dates for each task
- Logical sequencing of activities
- Realistic completion within April 1 - November 13, 2026

**Comprehensive Budget:**
- Itemized expenses clearly connected to activities
- Realistic cost estimates (with quotes for items ≥$5,000)
- Cost-effectiveness and value for money
- Adherence to activity-specific funding caps
- Clear budget justification for each line item

**Feasibility:**
- Achievable scope given timeline and resources
- Organizational capacity to deliver
- Risk awareness and mitigation strategies
- Clear roles and responsibilities

**Quality of Planning:**
- Professional presentation
- Attention to detail
- Integration between activities
- Contingency planning

### High-Scoring Strategy:

**Timeline Best Practices:**

✅ **Create Month-by-Month Schedule:**
```
April 2026:
- Finalize booth design and graphics (Week 1-2)
- Order marketing collateral materials (Week 2)
- Confirm retail demo locations (Week 3-4)

May 2026:
- Receive printed materials (Week 1)
- Launch social media campaign (Week 1)
- Conduct first round of in-store demos (Weeks 2-4)

[Continue through November 2026]
```

✅ **Include Specific Milestones:**
- April 15: Graphics and booth materials ordered
- May 1: Social media campaign launch
- May 15-30: First wave of in-store demos (10 locations)
- June 10: Tradeshow participation (WFIA Connect)
- July 1: Mid-project evaluation and adjustments
- August 15-17: BC Food & Beverage Show
- November 13: Final demos and project completion

✅ **Budget Justification for EVERY Line Item:**

**Example:**
"In-store demos (10 events @ $350 each = $3,500): Professional demo staff will conduct 4-hour sampling events at major Metro Vancouver grocery stores (Save-On-Foods, Thrifty Foods, etc.), distributing 200 samples per event and collecting consumer feedback. This directly supports Objective 2 (increase consumer awareness) and is expected to generate 2,000+ consumer touchpoints and 15% sales lift based on past demo performance."

**DON'T:**
❌ Provide vague timeline ("Summer 2026")
❌ List budget items without explanation
❌ Include unrealistic or padded costs
❌ Exceed activity-specific caps ($10K labelling, $5K collateral)
❌ Propose more activities than organizationally feasible

### Budget Optimization Tips:

**1. Show Cost-Effectiveness:**
- Explain how you're getting good value (e.g., "Bulk printing reduces per-unit cost to $0.75 vs. market rate of $1.20")
- Bundle services where possible
- Leverage existing relationships for better pricing

**2. Connect Budget to Outcomes:**
- For every expense, show expected impact
- "This $2,000 booth rental will generate 500+ qualified trade leads worth estimated $50,000 in annual sales"

**3. Demonstrate Financial Prudence:**
- Competitive pricing (show you shopped around)
- Realistic estimates (not underestimated)
- Clear tracking and accountability systems

**4. Use Quotes Strategically:**
- Get professional quotes for items ≥$5,000
- Include itemized breakdowns
- Show you've done due diligence

### Timeline Template:

**Month 1 (April 2026):**
| Week | Activity | Deliverable | Budget |
|------|----------|-------------|--------|
| 1-2 | Design & finalize booth graphics | Graphics files approved | $1,200 |
| 2-3 | Order marketing collateral | 5,000 brochures ordered | $3,750 |
| 3-4 | Confirm retail partnerships | 10 demo locations secured | $0 |

[Repeat for each month through November]

### Common Mistakes:
- Insufficient detail in timeline
- Vague budget descriptions
- Missing quotes for items ≥$5,000
- Unrealistic timeline compression
- Activities bunched at beginning or end
- No connection between budget and outcomes
- Poor budget justifications

### Word Count: Maximum 2000 words
**Recommended:** 1,800-2,000 words for comprehensive work plan

## Criterion 3: Expected Project Impact (25 points)

### What Reviewers Are Looking For:

**Quantifiable Outcomes:**
- Specific, measurable results
- Clear targets with baselines
- Methods for tracking/measuring
- Realistic projections based on data

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Activity-specific KPIs (aligned with program guidelines)
- Baseline → Target progression clearly stated
- Multiple KPIs across different impact areas
- Measurement methodology explained

**Long-Term Sustainability:**
- Impact extends beyond project period
- Building lasting partnerships or capacity
- Creating reusable marketing assets
- Establishing new distribution channels

**Economic Impact:**
- Sales growth projections
- New retail partnerships/accounts
- Jobs supported or created
- Contribution to BC agriculture/food sector growth

**Market Penetration:**
- Geographic reach within BC
- Consumer awareness increase
- Brand recognition growth
- Retail shelf space expansion

### High-Scoring Strategy:

**KPI Framework:**

For each activity, provide KPIs with:
1. **Baseline** (current state)
2. **Target** (goal by Nov 2026)
3. **Measurement method**
4. **Rationale** (why this target is achievable)

**Example KPI Table:**

| KPI | Baseline (March 2026) | Target (November 2026) | Measurement | Rationale |
|-----|----------------------|------------------------|-------------|-----------|
| Retail locations carrying products | 15 stores | 25 stores (+67%) | Retail partnership agreements | Based on 10 confirmed retailer meetings and past 50% conversion rate |
| Monthly sales volume | 2,000 units | 3,500 units (+75%) | Sales records | Historical 15% lift from demos × 10 events + tradeshow leads |
| Consumer awareness (aided recall) | 18% (baseline survey) | 35% (+17 points) | Post-campaign survey (n=500) | Advertising reach of 100K+ impressions + in-store presence |
| Website traffic | 800 visits/month | 1,600 visits/month (+100%) | Google Analytics | Campaign drives 50K+ ad impressions with 2% CTR |

**Activity-Specific KPI Examples:**

**Paid Advertising KPIs:**
- Views/reach: "Achieve 150,000 impressions across digital and print channels"
- Click-through rate: "2.5% CTR on digital ads (3,750 website visits)"
- Website traffic increase: "100% increase in monthly visitors (800 → 1,600)"
- Cost per impression: "$0.02 CPI (highly efficient for food marketing)"

**In-Store Demos KPIs:**
- Consumers engaged: "2,000+ consumers engaged across 10 demo events"
- Product samples distributed: "2,000 samples (200 per event)"
- Sales lift: "15% sales increase during demo periods based on retailer data"
- Conversion rate: "8% of consumers engaged make purchase (160 new customers)"

**Labelling/Packaging KPIs:**
- New products with Buy BC logo: "3 SKUs newly featuring Buy BC logo"
- Units produced: "10,000 labels printed and applied to products"
- Retail visibility: "Products placed in Buy BC sections of 20 stores"
- Sales increase: "12% sales lift on labeled vs. unlabeled SKUs (based on AB testing)"

**Trade Show KPIs:**
- Trade leads collected: "100+ qualified retail/distributor leads"
- New partnerships: "5 new retail partnership agreements signed"
- Product listings: "15 new SKUs listed with retail partners"
- Sales value: "$75,000 in new annual sales agreements"

**Marketing Collateral KPIs:**
- Materials produced: "5,000 brochures, 20 banners, 1 video"
- Distribution: "Materials distributed to 25 retail partners and 2,000 consumers"
- Engagement: "Video reaches 50,000 views on social media"

### Long-Term Impact Statement:

**Example:**
"This project establishes sustainable infrastructure for ongoing Buy BC promotion:
- **Reusable Assets:** Marketing materials, video, and booth graphics usable for 2+ years
- **Retail Relationships:** New partnerships create permanent shelf space for BC products
- **Consumer Awareness:** Increased brand recognition drives ongoing purchase behavior
- **Sales Systems:** Distribution channels established support long-term growth
- **Economic Impact:** Projected $100K+ in sustained annual sales increase supporting 2 FTE jobs"

### Common Mistakes:
- Vague outcomes without numbers
- KPIs without baselines or targets
- Unrealistic projections not supported by data
- Missing measurement methodology
- No long-term sustainability discussion
- Focus only on your business (not sector impact)

### Word Count: Maximum 1000 words
**Recommended:** 900-1,000 words with detailed KPI tables

## Criterion 4: Past Reporting/Performance (10 points)

### What Reviewers Are Looking For:

**First-Time Applicants:**
- ✅ Automatically receive full 10 points
- No past performance to evaluate
- Treated equally with strong past performers

**Returning Recipients (Funded 2023+):**
- Timely submission of previous final reports
- Achievement of stated objectives from past projects
- Financial accountability and proper use of funds
- Quality and impact of previous Buy BC activities
- Compliance with program requirements

### High-Scoring Strategy for Returning Recipients:

**DO:**
✅ Describe specific achievements from past projects
✅ Quantify results: "Exceeded sales target by 120%" or "Secured 12 new retail partnerships vs. 8 goal"
✅ Show timely reporting: "Submitted all reports on time with comprehensive documentation"
✅ Demonstrate financial accountability: "100% of funds used for approved activities with full documentation"
✅ Highlight quality outcomes: "Project resulted in sustained 25% sales increase still ongoing"
✅ Show learning and improvement: "Lessons from 2024 project inform enhanced 2026 approach"

**Example Past Performance Statement:**
"In our 2024 Buy BC project, we exceeded all targets:
- Sales Increase: Achieved 28% growth vs. 20% target
- Retail Expansion: Added 8 new stores vs. 5 goal
- Reporting: Submitted interim and final reports on time
- Financial Accountability: 100% compliant, all funds properly documented
- Lasting Impact: Partnerships and sales growth continue 12 months later
This track record demonstrates our capacity to deliver and exceed objectives."

**DON'T:**
❌ Ignore past performance (reviewers will check)
❌ Make excuses for missed objectives
❌ Blame external factors for poor performance
❌ Omit reporting issues or delays

### If Past Performance Was Weak:

**Be honest and show improvement:**
"While our 2023 project achieved 85% of sales targets due to supply chain challenges, we learned valuable lessons about timeline buffer and supplier diversification. Our 2026 project incorporates these learnings with:
- Earlier activity start dates
- Backup supplier agreements
- More conservative targets with contingency planning
We submitted all reports on time and achieved strong quality outcomes despite the challenges."

### Common Mistakes:
- Ignoring past performance entirely
- Making claims not supported by previous reports
- Defensive or excuse-heavy responses
- No demonstration of learning from past projects

## Final Merit Optimization Tips

### Maximize Your Score:

1. **Invest 40% of your effort in Work Plan section** (it's 40% of score)
   - Most detailed timeline possible
   - Comprehensive budget justifications
   - Professional presentation

2. **Use Specific Numbers Everywhere**
   - Objectives: specific targets
   - Budget: itemized costs
   - Impact: quantified outcomes
   - Timeline: exact dates

3. **Show, Don't Tell**
   - Don't say "significant increase" → Say "25% increase"
   - Don't say "many retailers" → Say "12 retail partners across Metro Vancouver"
   - Don't say "soon" → Say "May 15, 2026"

4. **Connect Everything**
   - Activities → Objectives
   - Budget → Outcomes
   - KPIs → Objectives
   - Timeline → Feasibility

5. **Demonstrate Professionalism**
   - Proofread thoroughly
   - Use tables and formatting for clarity
   - Provide evidence and quotes
   - Show you've done your homework

6. **Align with Buy BC Mission**
   - Explicitly reference program goals
   - Emphasize BC market focus
   - Highlight logo prominence
   - Show sector-wide benefit

**Remember: Reviewers are looking for well-planned, achievable projects that will successfully promote BC products in the BC market. Show them you have a solid plan, realistic expectations, and the capacity to deliver results!**
