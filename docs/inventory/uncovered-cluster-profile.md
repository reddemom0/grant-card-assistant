**Recommendation:** treat as **one family with three roles** — *form* (blank), *submission*
(filed), *confirmation* (acknowledged) — rather than collapsing to a single name. Roughly 700
files across the family.

they are a client-naming artifact, and the pattern `<Legal Name>-<Reference>-<Date>` is already a

**Dates are far more common than sequence numbers.** `claim submission` carries a date in 99% of

interchangeable — `Claim 1` for a client with two grant cycles is ambiguous, a period is not.
