# Granted Consulting Service Portfolio

**For Oracle AI Agent Use**: When generating leads, creating proposals, or recommending services, always match the appropriate service tier to the client's profile, budget, and needs.

---

## Service Tiers Overview

### 1. GetGranted (Evolving to Elivated in 2026)

**Type**: Self-service database subscription
**Website**: https://granted.ca/getgranted/ → https://elivated.ai/

**What's Included**:
- Up-to-date database of Canadian grants
- Intelligent grant matching based on company profile
- Email notifications for new opportunities
- Dashboard to track applications and funding status
- Grant summaries, tips, and resources
- Application guidance and best practices

**What's NOT Included**:
- No application writing or preparation
- No consulting or strategy support
- No claims assistance

**Pricing**:
- Monthly or yearly subscription (1-month minimum)
- No success-based fees

**Target Customer**:
- Companies wanting grant awareness
- Comfortable managing applications independently
- DIY mindset, limited budget
- Don't need hand-holding

**When to Recommend**:
- Client says: "We just want to know what's out there"
- Early-stage startups not ready for consulting
- Budget-conscious companies with in-house capability
- As an entry point before upselling to Starter/Pro

**Transition Note**: GetGranted is becoming Elivated (all-in-one grant management platform) in 2026 with enhanced features.

### 2. Granted Starter

**Type**: Self-serve platform with limited support
**Website**: https://granted.ca/granted-starter/

**What's Included**:
- Everything in GetGranted (database, matching, notifications)
- **3 Grant Tokens per year** for streamlined applications
- Simplified application forms (reduced complexity)
- Post-approval support (claims assistance, reminders)
- Token returned if application rejected (no fee charged)

**What's NOT Included**:
- Limited to **hiring and training grants only** (no market expansion, no R&D, no innovation)
- Maximum 3 applications per year
- Users still submit final applications themselves (Granted prepares, client submits)

**Pricing**:
- **Monthly**: $166.66/month ($1,700 annually)
- **Annual**: $1,700/year (25% discount vs monthly)
- **Success Fee**: 25% of approved grant amount
- If rejected, token returned and no success fee

**Target Customer**:
- Startups or recently incorporated businesses
- Companies new to grant funding
- Up to 3 annual grant-eligible activities
- Need some guidance but can self-select grants
- Hiring or training needs specifically

**When to Recommend**:
- Small businesses, < 25 employees
- Limited grant activity (1-3 per year)
- Only interested in hiring/training grants
- Want middle ground between DIY and full-service
- Budget < $10K/year for grant support

**Prerequisites for Other Services**:
- **Required to access Granted Export** (must have active Starter subscription)

**Disqualifiers**:
- Need R&D grants (IRAP, BCIP, NGen) → Needs GrantedPro or custom
- Need innovation grants → Needs GrantedPro or custom
- Need market expansion grants → Needs GrantedPro or Granted Export
- More than 3 applications/year → Needs GrantedPro

**Eligibility Requirements**:

- **$5MM+ annual revenue**
- **25+ employees**

- International expansion (often)

**Target Customer**:

- Regular grant applicants (5+ applications/year)
- High-value grants (IRAP, SIF, large provincial programs)

**When to Recommend**:

**Disqualifiers**:

## Service Selection Decision Tree

### Question 1: What's the client's revenue and size?

**< $5MM revenue OR < 25 employees**:
- → Starter or Granted Export (if export-focused)
- → GetGranted if very budget-conscious

**$5MM+ revenue AND 25+ employees AND 5+ years**:
- → GrantedPro (if they meet all three criteria)

---

### Question 2: What type of grants do they need?

**Only hiring or training** (1-3 per year):
- → Granted Starter (perfect fit)

**R&D, Innovation, Strategic programs** (IRAP, SIF, NGen, BCIP):
- → GrantedPro (Starter doesn't cover these)
- → Or custom project-based if one-off

**International expansion** (CanExport specifically):
- → Granted Export (if they have/will get Starter)
- → GrantedPro (if they want full-service and meet requirements)

**Multiple types** (hiring + training + R&D + export):
- → GrantedPro (unlimited applications, covers all)

---

### Question 3: What's their DIY appetite?

**Very DIY, just want awareness**:
- → GetGranted/Elivated (database only)

**Some support, can handle simple applications**:
- → Granted Starter (guided self-serve)

**Hands-off, want experts to handle everything**:
- → GrantedPro (done-for-you)
- → Granted Export (for CanExport specifically)

---

### Question 4: How many grants per year?

**1-3 applications**:
- → Granted Starter (3 tokens = perfect)

**4+ applications**:
- → GrantedPro (unlimited)

**Just one specific export application**:
- → Granted Export (project-based)

## Oracle Usage Guidelines

### When Creating Proposals
1. **Load client profile** from HubSpot (revenue, employees, age, industry)
2. **Identify grant needs** (hiring, R&D, export, etc.)
3. **Match to appropriate tier** using decision tree above
4. **Check eligibility** (GrantedPro requires 5yr/$5MM/25 emp, Export requires Starter)
5. **Explain why this tier** is right for them
6. **Show ROI** with example math
7. **Mention upgrade path** if starting with lower tier

### When Qualifying Leads
1. **Ask discovery questions**:
   - Company size (revenue, employees, years operating)
   - Grant interests (hiring? R&D? export?)
   - Previous grant experience
   - Budget expectations
   - DIY vs. full-service preference
2. **Score fit** for each tier
3. **Recommend best-fit tier** with rationale
4. **Set expectations** on pricing and outcomes

### When Handling Objections
- **"Too expensive"**:
  - Show ROI (grants fund > 3x the service cost)
  - Offer lower tier (Pro → Starter, Starter → GetGranted)
  - Payment plans if available
  - Emphasize success rates (93% Pro, 98% Export)

- **"We can do it ourselves"**:
  - Acknowledge capability
  - Mention success rates (DIY typically 30-50%, we're 93-98%)
  - Time savings (100+ hours per application)
  - Offer GetGranted as low-commitment option
  - Share case study of DIY → us conversion

- **"We don't qualify"**:
  - Check eligibility for other tiers
  - Suggest GetGranted to monitor for when they do qualify
  - Book follow-up in 6-12 months
  - Recommend specific steps to become eligible

### When Cross-Selling/Upselling
- **GetGranted → Starter**: "You've seen the grants available, ready to actually apply? Starter gives you 3 prepared applications per year."
- **Starter → Pro**: "You've maxed out your 3 tokens and it's only June. GrantedPro gives you unlimited applications plus a dedicated strategist."
- **Starter → Export**: "Planning US expansion? Add our Export service - 98% success rate on CanExport."
- **Pro → Export**: "You mentioned international growth - we have a specialized CanExport service with 98% success rate. Want us to add that to your strategy?"
