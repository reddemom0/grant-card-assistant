# GetGrantedAI — Strategic Program Intelligence

This document captures program-specific intelligence extracted from consulting call transcripts. It lives alongside the Strategic Consulting Skill (which captures consultant thinking patterns) and feeds INTO program cards as they're built.

---

## How to Use This Document

**Workflow:**
1. New call transcripts are processed → program-specific intelligence is extracted and added to the relevant program section below
2. When a program card is being built, pull the intelligence from this document into the card's Insider Knowledge, Timing Strategy, and FAQ sections
3. Once intelligence has been incorporated into a program card, mark it as `[INCORPORATED → card-id]` but don't delete it — the raw consulting context is sometimes richer than what fits in a card

**What belongs here:**
- Timing strategies, application tactics, budget approaches specific to a program
- Gotchas, exclusions, rule changes consultants have observed
- Success rates, typical approval amounts, processing timelines from experience
- What's covered / not covered that surprises clients
- Edge cases and classification nuances
- Insider knowledge from grantor relationships

**What does NOT belong here:**
- Consultant thinking patterns (→ Strategic Consulting Skill)
- Official eligibility criteria you can find on the program website (→ program card, sourced from guidelines)
- Application form fields and writing guidance (→ writing cards, sourced from forms)

**Source tracking:** Each entry notes which transcript batch it came from. Format: `[T#]` = transcript number from extraction sessions.

## CanExport SME (Market Expansion)

**Card status:** No card yet — priority build

**Headline stats from consulting experience:**
- Up to $50K, 50% coverage, international market expansion [T3, T5, T6, T8]
- $31M total program budget [T10]
- 92% Granted success rate overall; 95% on CanExport normally; 72% in one "shift year" when government changed rules mid-cycle; 100% by one consultant's count last year [T3, T8, T10]
- First-time applicants typically receive $25-30K, not the full $50K [T10]

**Timing intelligence:**
- Intake typically closes in May. Feb/Mar submissions approved by June. Apr/May submissions not approved until November. Earlier = faster. [T3]
- Activities must occur April 1 - March 31 of the funding year [T10]
- Don't put deposits down before application or before April 1 — once expenses are committed, those items can't be included [T10, T11]
- If approved after booking travel, they'll still reimburse — but if NOT approved, you're out of pocket [T11]
- Processing officially 60-90 business days but reality is longer for late submissions [T3]

**Budget and application strategy:**
- Always submit for more than you need — grantors will cut items. "It's like CRA — they find one thing to cut." If you submit exactly what you need and they cut one thing, you're under threshold. [T10]
- Longer applications with detailed market research perform better — Granted's applications are reportedly 4-5x longer than typical submissions [V1]
- Allows up to 5 markets per year but consultants recommend focusing on 2-3 for application strength [T5]
- Vague applications get rejected immediately — need demonstrated market knowledge and a concrete plan [V1]

**US market dynamics:**
- Only ~10% of $31M budget allocated to US projects — deprioritized due to "global changes" [T2, T6, T10]
- US is not impossible — strong unique products (e.g., FDA-approved innovations) can still make a case [T6]
- Previous years split US into 4 sub-markets (West, South, Northeast, Midwest); now consolidated into one. Reduces competition from existing US exporters who hopped between sub-regions. New-to-market applicants benefit. [T10]
- DC and below = South; above DC = Northeast; everything else = Midwest [V1]

**What's covered that people miss:**
- Legal/IP protection and trademark costs for export markets [T10]
- Taxation advisory for cross-border expansion [T10]
- Contractor-based lead generators in target market [T10]
- Event room rentals near trade shows (not the show itself, but adjacent events) [T10]
- Travel for buyer store tours in target markets [T10]
- Booth construction (rare, counted as equipment) [V1]

**What's NOT covered (removed last year):**
- Digital marketing/ad spend [T10]
- Food and alcohol at events (venue rental IS covered) [V1]
- Existing contractors already listed on your website/LinkedIn — viewed as "operational" not "incremental" [V1]
- Google/Facebook/LinkedIn ads that work in any English-speaking country — not market-specific enough [V1]

**Key rules and gotchas:**
- Target market must have <$100K in sales and <10% of revenue [T5, T6]
- Revenue threshold is $100K+ on last CRA T2 and GST return [V1]
- Youth entrepreneur and women entrepreneur are checkboxes — help with demographic distribution [T10]
- Contingency fees clause in application — geared toward government-connected people, not grant consultants [T10]
- Rules change mid-cycle — government sometimes changes criteria AFTER program opens [T10]
- Can be used repeatedly year after year for different markets; once you hit $100K in a market, that market becomes ineligible [V1]

**Trade show strategy:**
- Walk a show in year 1 to scope it; apply for booth costs in year 2 [T10]
- Include heavier-cost items like booth fees in the application budget [T10]

**Food & beverage EXCLUSION:**
- F&B production was removed from eligible categories in current intake — new change [T7]
- Major impact on craft spirits, food producers, beverage companies [T7]
- Consultant describes this as blocking "our absolute easiest avenue" for F&B export clients [T7]
- Pivot: redirect F&B clients to agri-food grants or specialist consultation [T7]

**Batch 2 additions:**

**Success rate updates:**
- Jorge cites 92% overall, 100% last year [B2-validere, B2-coastfraser]
- Steph cites 95% normally, 72% in shift year, 88% adjusted (one rejection was a documentation miss, not a write-up quality issue) [B2-cosmetic]
- Recreation context: ~90% average, "double the government success rate" [B2-recreation]

**US market — additional detail:**
- Government actively looking for companies expanding BEYOND US — having Europe/other markets planned strengthens any application [B2-cosmetic]
- Within the 10% US allocation, defense industry projects are prioritized [B2-talentmap]
- Non-defense US applications are "playing against the odds" — Jorge frames as potentially not worth pursuing [B2-talentmap]
- US previously split into 4 sub-markets; now consolidated into one — less competition for new-to-market companies, but also less opportunity to hop between sub-regions [B2-cosmetic]
- Companies with 50%+ US revenue are immediately disqualified from US market CanExport — redirect to non-US markets where revenue is under threshold [B2-coastland]

**Eligibility exclusions — new patterns:**
- Middleman/reseller exclusion: companies that buy, store, repack/rebrand, and re-export without Canadian value-add manufacturing are excluded [B2-coastfraser]
- Value-add manufacturing (even at a newly acquired facility) changes eligibility — acquiring a facility for value-add work would definitively qualify a previously excluded company [B2-coastfraser]
- Sole proprietorship eligibility: uncertain — Jorge needed to check internally. Open question. [B2-durfeld]
- Services ARE eligible: B2B software, consulting services, franchise operations, facility setup internationally all qualify [B2-validere, B2-recreation]
- Pop-up events must be connected to a trade show being attended — can't be standalone [B2-cosmetic]

**What's covered — additions:**
- Franchise expansion, facility setup abroad, distributor meetings all qualify as eligible activities [B2-recreation]
- Contractor-based lead generators and sales agencies in target market [B2-cosmetic]
- Taxation advisory and legal costs for cross-border compliance [B2-cosmetic]
- Room rental near trade show for private buyer events, table rentals [B2-cosmetic]
- Travel for retail store tours and buyer meetings [B2-cosmetic]

**What's NOT covered — additions:**
- Retainer-based vendor services — must restructure as project-based with deliverables and lump sum/hourly scope [B2-cosmetic]
- Food and alcohol at events (venue rental IS covered) [B2-cosmetic]

**Pricing/fees:**
- Writing fee for Pro clients: $3,000 upfront deposit against 20% success fee — deducted from final invoice [B2-cosmetic, B2-validere]
- Writing fee for Starter clients: flat $6,000 (not success-based) [B2-cosmetic]
- Success fee above $50K threshold: decreases on case-by-case basis. Example: 10% for $1M RTRI application [B2-talentmap, B2-heyya]

**Timing — additions:**
- Program allows up to 5 markets, but Jorge recommends max 3 for a strong application [B2-validere]
- Best case processing: apply February → first round approvals May [B2-validere]
- Last day to apply: May 29 (2026 cycle) [B2-recreation]
- If flights/hotel booked before approval but applicant IS approved, expenses still reimbursed. If not approved, out of pocket. If booked before APPLICATION, those items can't be included. [B2-recreation]

**Consultant escalation pattern:**
- Borderline CanExport cases (US-only expansion, middleman classification, sole proprietor eligibility) are escalated to CEO/executive team rather than guessed at on the call [B2-talentmap, B2-coastfraser]

## RTRI — Regional Tariff Relief Initiative

**Card status:** No card yet

- EOI (Expression of Interest) is less technical; full application is heavily technical [T5]
- Requires deep technical input from client's team — consultant can't do alone [T5]
- Larger grant amounts than standard programs [T5]
- Separate annex/contract for writing fees and success rates [T5]
- Writing fee of $3-4K for complex applications over $50K — deducted from success fee [T5]

**Core details:**
- Open since October; first round approved Dec/Jan; second round expected [T3]
- Two streams: non-repayable (up to $1M, 50% coverage) and repayable (up to $10M, favorable terms — ~1yr grace, 5-6yr payback, low/zero interest — better than bank) [T3]
- RETROACTIVE — covers costs incurred since April (rare for grants) [T3]
- Competitive, late in the cycle but still worth applying [T3]

**Eligible activities:**
- Market expansion within Canada, new facilities, market research, diversification — anything to reduce US reliance [T3]

**Requirements:**
- Must demonstrate tangible revenue impact from tariffs with year-over-year documentation [T3]
- Priority areas: technology, AI, defense, manufacturing, metals, natural resources [T3]

**Batch 2 additions:**

**Indirect tariff impact qualification:**
- Company that doesn't sell to US directly but exports to Vietnam (which ships finished goods to US) — contract cancellations from tariff cascading accepted as qualifying impact [B2-coastfraser]
- Creative framing of indirect supply chain impact is viable, not just direct US trade losses [B2-coastfraser]

**Application status:**
- "Provisionally approved" status exists — client has a government contact monitoring their application [B2-coastfraser]
- Complex, comprehensive application process confirmed by both client and consultant [B2-coastfraser]
- Jorge working on RTRI simultaneously for a BC wood manufacturer [B2-coastfraser, B2-robertson]

**Scale and fees:**
- $1M application level confirmed across multiple transcripts [B2-coastfraser, B2-heyya, B2-robertson]
- Success fee at this level: 10% [B2-heyya, B2-robertson, B2-talentmap]
- Up to $1M for non-repayable stream [B2-heyya]

**Card priority:** HIGH — appeared in 4+ transcripts with substantial intelligence. Multiple active applications in progress.

## Student Hiring Grants (Cross-Program)

**Card status:** Multiple cards exist (DS4Y, SWPP, IRAP YEP, WIL Digital, PLTC Green Jobs) — this section captures cross-cutting intelligence that applies to all of them

**Universal patterns:**
- "Lowest hanging fruit" — many programs, open year-round, various fields [T2, T3, T5]
- $5,000-$7,000 per student per term [T2, T3]
- Turnaround: ~2-3 weeks depending on program [T2]
- Can reapply for subsequent terms if student is rehired — must show incremental responsibilities (updated job description) [T4]
- Prioritize underrepresented groups (minorities, disabilities) for higher funding amounts [T2]

**Operational details:**
- Minimum hours: 15+ hours/week typical threshold for meaningful placements [T11]
- Field alignment required: student's academic program must align with the role [T11]
- Co-op students work too — doesn't have to be formal internship, just integrated with learning [T11]
- If student already on payroll before grant application, must justify retroactive placement — tricky but not impossible [T9]
- Multi-location flexibility: if all locations under one payroll, students can work across locations [T11]

**Sizing and scaling:**
- Scaling math: 4 students in summer + 2-3 rest of year × $5-7K each = $70-80K annual savings for multi-location business [T11]
- Senior students (MBAs, grad students) can be more effective — more deliverable-focused, less training overhead [T11]
- Kinesiology, business, economics, engineering students all mentioned as good fits across industries [T11]

**Timing:**
- Semester-based: May-Aug, Sep-Dec, Jan-Apr [V1]
- Winter term programs close; spring/summer programs open in weeks leading up to April (fiscal year alignment) [T4]
- If hiring in July, better to start the grant placement in September for full term coverage [V1]
- It's typically too late to get summer students placed if it's already May/June — plan for fall [V1]

**Batch 2 additions:**

**Eligibility detail:**
- Must be from recognized post-secondary institution (bachelor's, master's, PhD) [B2-papertail, B2-durfeld]
- High school students do NOT qualify for standard student programs — redirect to Canada Summer Jobs [B2-durfeld]
- Most programs require co-op letter from university — not all, but most [B2-papertail, B2-durfeld]
- Vetting criteria can change: "The program can change criteria tomorrow" — vet close to application time [B2-papertail]
- Granted acts as third-party buffer for eligibility vetting: programs require candidate characteristics (age, status, disability) that employers can't legally ask [B2-talentmap]

**Re-application mechanics:**
- Can reapply for same student with incremental responsibilities — stacks to $14K+ per student [B2-talentmap, B2-validere]
- Granted itself uses this: PhD student developing AI, two terms, $14K total [B2-validere, B2-coastland, B2-robertson]

**Retroactive hiring:**
- 80-90% of hiring grants are NOT retroactive — must apply before start date [B2-heyya]
- Exception exists: students already on payroll CAN qualify, but need justification for retroactive placement. Better to align start dates with April grant cycle [B2-recreation]
- Can hire student before being grant-eligible (under one year), then apply for grant on second term once eligible [B2-papertail]

**Sizing math examples:**
- 10 students × $5-7K = $50-70K per term. Half a year of 10 students = $100-150K [B2-heyya]
- 5 applications per term × $5-7K = at least $30K on low end [B2-coastland]
- 2 students × 4 terms × $5K = $30-40K annual [B2-validere]
- 4 students summer + 2-3 rest of year × $5-7K = $70-80K annual [B2-recreation]

**Industry-specific notes:**
- Accounting students for bookkeeping firms: confirmed eligible. Case study: Kamloops accounting firm, 2-3 students placed in December [B2-papertail]
- Forestry: programs always have funds — Jorge hasn't seen a situation where forestry student applications couldn't be submitted [B2-robertson]
- Hospitality/tourism: digital marketing students and hospitality students both eligible [B2-restaurant]
- Tech: easiest category. Granted itself hires students for AI development [B2-validere]

**Canada Summer Jobs (separate from standard student programs):**
- Ages 15-30, temporary workers — broader than post-secondary student programs [B2-durfeld]
- Up to 50% of provincial minimum wage for up to 16 weeks [B2-durfeld]
- Realistically: $8-11K per hire [B2-durfeld]
- Opens annually — just closed at time of March 2026 calls [B2-durfeld]
- Popular with forestry clients for seasonal summer hires [B2-durfeld]
- Lower requirements than post-secondary programs — good fit for trades/labor roles [B2-durfeld]

## IFIT — Investments in Forest Industry Transformation

**Card status:** No card yet — priority build for forestry vertical

**Core details:**
- Up to $5M grant (50% of total project cost, so total project up to $10M) [B2-durfeld, B2-robertson]
- Capital costs ARE eligible — part of the new wave of capital cost programs [B2-robertson]
- Provided by NRCan [B2-coastland]
- Just opened (early March 2026) [B2-robertson]

**Five categories:**
1. **Advanced wood-based building solutions** — most important category. Innovative structural/architectural solutions designed to displace carbon-intensive materials (concrete, steel, fossil-based insulation) [B2-durfeld, B2-robertson, B2-coastland]
2. **Biomaterials and biochemicals** [B2-robertson]
3. **Pulp and paper modernization** [B2-robertson]
4. **Next generation biofuels** [B2-robertson]
5. **Advanced decarbonization** [B2-robertson]

**Common disqualifiers (from 3 forestry calls):**
- Companies making industrial products (survey stakes, shipping crates, mining supplies) don't fit unless they can tie to housing or decarbonization narratives [B2-robertson]
- Raw material suppliers providing inputs to other manufacturers (veneer, peelers for plywood) don't naturally fit — program targets finished building solutions and innovation, not upstream supply chain [B2-coastland]
- Companies without housing/construction output have tried creative angles (shipping crates for gas infrastructure → "decarbonization") but these don't hold up when the client reality-checks them [B2-robertson]

**Good fit for:**
- Log and timber building companies doing innovative wall systems and fire testing [B2-durfeld]
- Companies partnering with First Nations on modular wood housing [B2-durfeld]
- Any company producing finished wood-based building products that displace concrete/steel [B2-coastland]

**Related programs:**
- Northern BC Forest Innovation Fund: up to $50K or 50% for SMEs in northern regions — applied R&D, new/improved products, testing innovative equipment [B2-durfeld]
- Build Canada Homes: referenced by client, related to modular wood housing — needs investigation [B2-durfeld]
