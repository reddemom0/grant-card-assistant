# DS4Y - Pinnguaq (Digital Skills for Youth)
**Program ID:** ds4y-pinnguaq
**Type:** Hiring
**Province:** Canada-wide
**Status:** Active (runs in fiscal year cycles)

---

## Program Overview

DS4Y (Digital Skills for Youth) is administered by Pinnguaq Association and provides wage subsidies for Canadian employers hiring youth (ages 15-30) for digital skills-focused placements. The program follows a fiscal year cycle (April-March) with Excel-based claim/advance submission system. Funding is distributed through advance payments and final reconciliation claims.

**Portal:** Application-based (PDF employer application forms)
**Administrator:** Pinnguaq Association (IHO - Indigenous Host Organization)

---

## Eligibility

### Employer Eligibility
- Must be a Canadian business, non-profit, or organization
- Must be able to pay employee wages in full before reimbursement
- Must commit to consistent hours for the placement duration
- Must provide digital skills training/development opportunities
- Cannot be federal, provincial, or territorial government departments

### Candidate Eligibility
- **Age:** 15-30 years old
- **Citizenship:** Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or person granted refugee status
- **Employment Status:** Unemployed or underemployed youth
- **Skills Focus:** Placement must develop digital skills (web development, digital marketing, data analysis, software development, digital content creation, etc.)
- Must complete Participant Information Form (PIF)

### Red Flags / Likely Rejections
- Candidate over 30 years old
- Role does not involve meaningful digital skills development
- Employer cannot afford to pay wages upfront
- Placement already started before approval
- Government department applying directly

### Stacking Rules
- Cannot stack with other federal wage subsidy programs for the same employee during the same period
- May be combined with training grants (verify with program administrator)
- Check provincial wage subsidy stacking rules

## Vetting Questions

1. **Is the candidate between 15-30 years old?** — Core eligibility requirement
2. **Does the role involve digital skills development?** — Must be central to the position
3. **Can you pay the employee's full wages before receiving reimbursement?** — Employer must cashflow wages
4. **Can you commit to consistent hours for the full placement period?** — Stability requirement
5. **Has the placement already started?** — Must not have started before approval
6. **What digital skills will the candidate develop?** — Need specific examples (e.g., "Python programming", "SEO/SEM", "UI/UX design")
7. **Is this a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or refugee?** — Citizenship requirement

## Application Process

### Strategy Team Steps (Before GC)
1. **Vet Candidate Age** — Confirm 15-30 years old
2. **Vet Job Description** — Ensure digital skills are central, save to Dropbox with resume
3. **Confirm Employer Capacity** — Can they cashflow wages?
4. **Determine Region** — Select correct application form version (standard vs. Northern)
5. **Fill Grant Planner** — Calculate subsidy estimates
6. **Prepare Application Package** — Employer application, JD, resume
7. **Move to Pending Submission** — Assign to GC

### Grant Coordinator Steps

**Step 1: Prepare Application**
- Use correct Employer Application form (check region)
- Complete all employer information sections
- Attach job description and candidate resume
- Submit application to Pinnguaq (check current submission method - likely email)

**Step 2: Participant Information Forms**
- Once conditionally accepted, Pinnguaq will provide PIF form
- Send PIF to candidate to complete
- Candidate submits directly to Pinnguaq or returns to GC for forwarding
- **Application not complete until PIFs received**

**Step 3: Contribution Agreement Review**
- Pinnguaq issues Contribution Agreement (CA)
- Review Part I (standard terms) and Part II Appendix (company-specific)
- Confirm: placement dates, wage amount, subsidy rate, reporting requirements
- Client signs and returns CA
- Keep signed copy in Dropbox

**Step 4: Track Approval**
- Monitor for final approval letter
- Placement can start only after CA fully executed
- Update HubSpot with approval date and placement start date

## Claims

### What's Included
- **Wages** — Eligible wages paid to the participant
- **Mandatory Employment-Related Costs (MERCs)** — CPP, EI, EHT, WSIB, vacation pay (verify with program)
- **Training Expenses** — Some programs allow training costs (verify with Pinnguaq)

### What's NOT Included
- Wages paid before placement start date
- Wages paid after placement end date
- Bonuses or incentive payments (verify)
- Benefits beyond statutory requirements
- Equipment or supplies

### Claim Process

**Claim Types:**
1. **Advance Claims** — Submitted during placement for partial reimbursement
2. **Final Claim** — Submitted at placement completion for full reconciliation

**Steps:**
1. **Download Template** — Use correct fiscal year version:
   - 2020-2021: v1.1, v1.2
   - 2021-2022: v1.3
   - 2023-2024: Latest version (check with Pinnguaq)

2. **Complete Claim Sheet** (Excel):
   - Enter all pay periods
   - Attach paystubs for each period
   - Calculate wages, hours, MERCs
   - Save as: [CompanyName]_DS4Y-Claim-Advance-Sheet-[FiscalYear]-[Version]-Template.xlsx

3. **Attach Supporting Documents:**
   - All paystubs (PDF)
   - Training receipts if applicable
   - QPR form if due with claim

4. **Submit to Pinnguaq:**
   - Email claim package (check CA for submission email)
   - Confirm receipt
   - Track claim status

5. **Claim Review:**
   - Pinnguaq reviews claim
   - May request corrections or additional documentation
   - Approve and issue payment

**Claim Frequency:**
- Advance claims: Likely quarterly or mid-placement (check CA)
- Final claim: Within 30 days of placement end (verify timeline in CA)

## Financial Calculations

### Subsidy Rate
- **Wage subsidy percentage:** Varies by fiscal year and region (typically 50-75% of wages)
- **Maximum per participant:** Check current fiscal year guidelines
- Check Contribution Agreement for exact subsidy rate for your placement

### MERCs Calculation
- Use standard CRA rates for:
  - CPP (employer portion)
  - EI (employer portion)
  - Provincial payroll taxes (EHT where applicable)
  - WSIB (if applicable)
  - Vacation pay (typically 4%)

### Claim Sheet Calculations
- Excel template auto-calculates if formulas intact
- Verify wage × hours matches paystubs
- Verify MERC calculations match CRA rates
- Cross-check totals before submission

## Program-Specific Rules & Quirks

- **Excel-Based System** — Unlike portal programs, DS4Y uses downloadable Excel claim templates. No online portal for claims.

- **Version Control Critical** — Multiple template versions exist by fiscal year. Using wrong version may cause claim rejection. Always verify you have the correct year's template.

- **Contribution Agreement Variations:**
  - "Southern Part I" = Standard agreement for most of Canada
  - Separate versions for Northern territories (NU/NWT/YK)
  - Part II Appendix is company-specific

- **IHO (Indigenous Host Organization)** — Pinnguaq is the IHO administering this program. References to "IHO" in forms refer to Pinnguaq.

- **Additional Placements** — Can add more participants to existing agreement using "DS4Y Pinnguaq Additional Placements Form"

- **Fiscal Year Cycles** — Program runs April-March. Applications submitted in Q4 (Jan-Mar) may be for following fiscal year.

- **QPR Timing** — Quarterly Progress Reports may be required. Check CA for specific deadlines (not always exactly quarterly).

- **Advance vs. Final Claims** — Same Excel template used for both. Mark clearly which type of claim you're submitting.

---

## Best Practices & Warnings

- **Keep Blank Templates** — Save blank template separate from filled versions. Don't accidentally overwrite.

- **Paystub Collection** — Start collecting from Day 1. Missing even one paystub delays claims.

- **Name Claim Files Clearly** — Use pattern: [Company]_DS4Y-Claim-Advance-Sheet-[Year]-v[Version]-[Advance/Final].xlsx

- **Check CA Before Every Claim** — Subsidy rates, eligible expenses, and reporting requirements are in the CA. Re-read before each claim.

- **Version Mismatch** — If you have v1.1 but Pinnguaq released v1.2 mid-year, ask which to use. They may require migration to newer version.

- **Northern Territories** — NU/NWT/YK have separate application forms and potentially different CA terms. Verify which version applies.

- **Digital Skills Must Be Real** — "Uses computer" is not enough. Role must develop specific digital competencies (coding, digital marketing, data analysis, etc.). Be specific in application.

- **Candidate Age at Start** — Candidate must be 30 or under when placement starts, not when you apply.

- **Employment Clarification** — Some files reference "Employment Clarification for Interns" doc. If candidate is intern vs. employee, confirm structure with Pinnguaq.

## Pay Stub Verification Checklist

- [ ] **Candidate Name** — Matches agreement
- [ ] **Hourly Rate** — Matches wage stated in CA
- [ ] **Hours Worked** — Falls within agreed weekly hours
- [ ] **Pay Period** — Falls within placement start and end dates in CA
- [ ] **Gross Pay** — Hours × Rate = Gross (verify calculation)
- [ ] **Deductions Present** — CPP, EI, Income Tax deducted (proves employment, not contractor)
- [ ] **Employer Name** — Matches company in agreement
- [ ] **Pay Date** — Reasonable (not backdated)
- [ ] **No Gaps** — All pay periods from start to current claim date included

**Red Flags:**
- Pay rate changes mid-placement without CA amendment
- No statutory deductions (indicates contractor, not employee)
- Hours significantly higher than agreed in CA
- Gaps in pay periods

## Form & Document Guidance (Client-Facing)

### DS4Y Employer Application
**Versions:**
- `DS4Y-EmployerApplication-ExcludingNUNWTYK-V1.pdf` — Standard (most of Canada)
- `DS4Y-EmployerApplication-ForNUNWTYK-V1.pdf` — Northern territories
- `DS4Y-Employer-Application-English-excluding-NU-NWT-YK-V2.1.pdf` — Updated version

**Key Sections:**
- Employer Information (legal name, address, business number)
- Participant Information (name, age, citizenship)
- Position Details (title, wage, hours, start/end dates)
- Digital Skills Description (be specific!)

### Participant Information Form (PIF)
- Provided by Pinnguaq after conditional acceptance
- Candidate completes directly
- Includes: personal info, education, employment history, consent
- Required before final approval

### Contribution Agreement (CA)
**Part I: Standard Terms**
- Legal obligations
- Reporting requirements
- Eligible/ineligible expenses
- Payment terms
- Termination clauses

**Part II: Appendix (Company-Specific)**
- Your company details
- Specific placement details (dates, wage, participant name)
- Subsidy amount and rate
- QPR schedule

**Review Carefully:**
- Placement dates — ensure realistic
- Wage amount — ensure affordable to cashflow
- Reporting deadlines — mark calendar

### DS4Y Claim Advance Sheet (Excel)
**File:** `[Company]_DS4Y-Claim-Advance-Sheet-[Year]-v[Version]-Template.xlsx`

**Tabs/Sections (vary by version):**
- Company Information
- Participant Information
- Pay Period Details (enter each pay period)
- Wage Calculations
- MERC Calculations
- Total Claim Amount

**Instructions:**
1. **Do not delete formulas** — Excel template has built-in calculations
2. **Enter each pay period separately** — One row per paystub
3. **Attach corresponding paystubs** — Must match entered data
4. **Double-check totals** — Verify auto-calculations make sense
5. **Save before submitting** — Keep copy for records

### Quarterly Progress Report (QPR) Form
**File:** `Pinnguaq-DS4Y-IHO-QPR-form.pdf` or `NEW Pinnguaq-DS4Y-IHO-QPR-form.pdf`

**Typical Questions:**
- How is the placement progressing?
- What digital skills has the participant developed?
- Any challenges or issues?
- Is participant on track to complete placement?

**Tips:**
- Be specific about skills development (not just "going well")
- Mention projects candidate has worked on
- Flag issues early (don't wait until final report)
