# CanExport Application Evaluation Rubric

**Purpose:** This rubric provides a structured framework for evaluating completed CanExport SME applications against the program's official assessment criteria. Use this to review draft applications before submission or to assess the strength of completed applications.

**Scoring System:** Each criterion is rated on a 4-point scale:
- **1 - Weak:** Significant gaps, lacks detail, or does not meet requirements
- **2 - Adequate:** Meets basic requirements but lacks depth or compelling justification
- **3 - Strong:** Well-justified, detailed, and clearly aligned with program criteria
- **4 - Excellent:** Exceptional presentation with compelling evidence and strategic alignment

---

## Overall Application Quality

Before diving into specific criteria, assess these foundational elements:

### Application Completeness
- [ ] All required sections completed
- [ ] All character limits respected (not significantly under or over)
- [ ] Supporting documents provided (GST/HST returns, Articles of Incorporation)
- [ ] No placeholder text or "TBD" entries
- [ ] Budget totals correctly calculated (50% split)
- [ ] Project dates realistic (start date at least 60 business days from submission)

### Clarity & Presentation
- [ ] Clear, concise writing without jargon
- [ ] Specific details provided (named events, actual dates, identified vendors)
- [ ] Consistent narrative across all sections
- [ ] No conflicting information between sections
- [ ] Professional tone throughout

### Red Flags (Immediate Concerns)
- [ ] Information unchanged from previous submission
- [ ] Vague or generic descriptions
- [ ] Unverifiable claims
- [ ] Arm's length violations (vendors affiliated with company)
- [ ] Ineligible expenses included
- [ ] Missing critical information

## CRITERION A: Incrementality (Pursuing New or Expanded Initiatives)

**Weight:** High Priority
**Key Question:** Does this project represent new or expanded international business development activities that go beyond core operations?

### Scoring Guide

#### 4 - Excellent
- Project clearly represents **new market entry** or **significant expansion** beyond current activities
- Strong narrative explaining how this differs from/builds on previous work
- Demonstrates strategic progression in international development
- For repeat applicants: Compelling explanation of how new project builds on previous success with different focus/approach
- Activities are clearly incremental (not business-as-usual)

**Evidence:**
- "This is our first entry into [market]"
- "While our previous project focused on X, this project advances our strategy by targeting Y"
- "These activities represent expansion beyond our current operations in [market]"

#### 3 - Strong
- Project represents expansion into new markets or new activities
- Clear distinction from ongoing business operations
- For repeat applicants: Adequate explanation of differences from previous project
- Activities are incremental with some strategic justification

**Evidence:**
- New market with reasonable justification
- New activity types not previously undertaken
- Evolution from previous strategy explained

#### 2 - Adequate
- Project includes some new elements but also routine activities
- Weak distinction from core business operations
- For repeat applicants: Limited explanation of how project differs from previous
- Incrementality not strongly demonstrated

**Red Flags:**
- Activities sound like regular business operations
- Repeat market with same activities as previous project
- No clear explanation of what's new or expanded

#### 1 - Weak
- Project appears to fund core business operations
- For repeat applicants: No explanation of differences from previous project
- Activities are routine/ongoing business expenses
- No clear incrementality

**Disqualifying Issues:**
- Identical to previous project with no changes explained
- Clearly funding operational expenses (salaries, office rent, etc.)

### Assessment Questions
1. Is this genuinely new or expanded activity, or routine business?
2. For repeat applicants: How does this build on/differ from previous projects?
3. Would the company undertake these activities anyway without funding?
4. Is there strategic progression in their international development?

**Score: ___/4**

## CRITERION B: Export Business Case

**Weight:** High Priority
**Key Question:** Is the project realistic and likely to achieve meaningful commercial outcomes?

This criterion is assessed across multiple application sections:
- Project Summary
- Capacity
- Market Study
- Activity Budget Descriptions
- Benefits to Canada

### Scoring Guide

#### 4 - Excellent
- **Objectives** are specific, measurable, and clearly aligned with company capabilities
- **Activities** are specific with named events, confirmed dates, identified vendors
- **Expected outcomes** are realistic with concrete targets (revenue, leads, agreements)
- **Activity justification** in budget section shows strategic thinking and clear ROI
- **Expenses** are detailed, reasonable, and directly tied to activities
- **Timeline** is realistic with appropriate sequencing
- Strong logical connection between activities and expected outcomes
- Evidence-based projections (conversion rates, market data, past performance)

**Evidence:**
- "We will attend [Named Event] on [Date] in [City]"
- "Based on our 5% conversion rate, we expect to generate 150 qualified leads"
- "Average contract value of $50K × 3 expected sales = $150K revenue"
- Detailed vendor profiles with URLs and specific scope of work
- Each activity has clear purpose and expected outcome

#### 3 - Strong
- Objectives are clear and mostly measurable
- Most activities are specific with some named events/vendors
- Expected outcomes are reasonable with some quantification
- Activity justification shows understanding of market
- Expenses are reasonable and mostly well-justified
- Timeline is realistic

**Evidence:**
- Most major activities have specific details
- Reasonable revenue/lead projections provided
- Clear connection between activities and goals

#### 2 - Adequate
- Objectives are stated but somewhat vague
- Activities lack specificity (e.g., "attend trade shows" without naming them)
- Expected outcomes are generic or unrealistic
- Activity justification is thin
- Some expenses seem inflated or poorly justified
- Timeline may be tight or unclear

**Red Flags:**
- Vague vendor descriptions ("we will hire a consultant")
- No specific event names or dates
- Unrealistic projections ("we expect $5M in sales from a $20K project")
- Activities don't logically connect to stated objectives
- Budget lacks detailed breakdowns

#### 1 - Weak
- Objectives are vague or unmeasurable
- Activities are generic with no specifics
- Expected outcomes are unrealistic or missing
- Little to no activity justification
- Expenses are poorly explained or seem unreasonable
- Timeline is unrealistic

**Disqualifying Issues:**
- Ineligible expenses included (monthly retainers, digital ads, website creation, influencer fees)
- Activities cannot be independently verified
- No clear business case for how activities will lead to outcomes

### Sub-Components to Assess

#### B1: Objectives Clarity & Realism
- [ ] Specific measurable targets provided
- [ ] Aligned with company size and capacity
- [ ] Realistic given timeline and budget
- [ ] Connected to activities proposed

**Score: ___/4**

#### B2: Activity Specificity & Reasonableness
- [ ] Named events with confirmed dates
- [ ] Identified vendors with website URLs
- [ ] Clear scope of work for each activity
- [ ] Appropriate sequencing and timing
- [ ] Activities address market-specific needs

**Score: ___/4**

#### B3: Expected Outcomes & ROI
- [ ] Quantified targets (leads, sales, agreements)
- [ ] Realistic based on industry benchmarks
- [ ] Clear methodology for projections
- [ ] Strong value proposition (outcomes justify investment)

**Score: ___/4**

#### B4: Budget Justification & Reasonableness
- [ ] Detailed cost breakdowns for each activity
- [ ] Expenses are market-appropriate
- [ ] No inflated costs
- [ ] 50% matching contribution clearly stated
- [ ] All expenses are eligible

**Score: ___/4**

### Assessment Questions
1. Could an adjudicator independently verify these activities (named events, real vendors)?
2. Do the expected outcomes seem achievable given the activities and budget?
3. Is there a clear logical flow: activities → outcomes → benefits?
4. Are the cost estimates reasonable for the market and activity type?

## CRITERION C: Market Potential

**Weight:** High Priority
**Key Question:** Has the applicant demonstrated commercial potential in their target market(s)?

Assessed primarily in: Market Study, Competition sections

#### 4 - Excellent
- **Market research** is thorough with specific data (market size, growth rates, trends)
- **Market entry rationale** is compelling and evidence-based
- **Commercial potential** is clearly demonstrated with supporting data
- **Opportunities** are specific and tied to market conditions
- **Target customer profile** is detailed and well-researched
- **Competitive analysis** shows deep market understanding
- **Risks identified** with credible mitigation strategies
- Multiple sources of evidence (market reports, trade data, industry analysis)

**Evidence:**
- "The UK spa market is valued at $2.3B with 8% CAGR"
- "Growing demand for eco-friendly products aligns with our value proposition"
- "Target demographic: 25-45 year old professionals, $75K+ income"
- Specific competitor analysis with market positioning
- Free trade agreement mentioned as advantage

#### 3 - Strong
- Market research shows good understanding
- Clear rationale for target market selection
- Commercial potential is demonstrated
- Target customer profile is identified
- Competition is analyzed
- Some risks identified with mitigation plans

**Evidence:**
- Market size and growth mentioned
- Clear opportunities identified
- Reasonable customer profile
- Competitive landscape understood

#### 2 - Adequate
- Basic market research provided
- Generic rationale for market selection
- Commercial potential is asserted but not strongly demonstrated
- Target customer profile is vague
- Limited competitive analysis
- Few risks identified or weak mitigation

**Red Flags:**
- No market data provided (just assertions)
- "We chose this market because it's big" (no deeper analysis)
- Generic opportunities that could apply to any market
- No competitor names or analysis
- Risks identified without mitigation strategies

#### 1 - Weak
- Little to no market research evident
- Weak or missing rationale for market selection
- Commercial potential is not demonstrated
- No clear target customer profile
- No competitive analysis
- Risks not identified

## CRITERION D: Exporting Readiness and History

**Weight:** High Priority
**Key Question:** Does the company have the capacity and track record to successfully execute this project?

Assessed primarily in: Capacity, Internal/External Contributors, Company Information sections

### Scoring Guide

#### 4 - Excellent
- **Human resources** clearly demonstrate capacity to execute project
  - Experienced team members with relevant skills
  - Dedicated project manager identified
  - Appropriate internal/external resource mix
- **Financial resources** are solid
  - Healthy revenue growth trajectory
  - Clear funding source for 50% contribution
  - Appropriate company size for project scale
- **Export history** shows readiness
  - Currently exporting to multiple markets OR
  - Strong domestic success positioning for export
  - Previous CanExport projects completed successfully (if applicable)
- **Product/service commercialization** is proven
  - Established customer base
  - Market-ready product/service
  - Evidence of demand (awards, recognition, existing sales)
- **TCS engagement** is active
  - Current relationship with Trade Commissioner Service
  - Engaged with NRC-IRAP or other partners

**Evidence:**
- "Our VP of International Sales has 15 years of export experience"
- "Revenue grew 40% YoY from $2M to $2.8M"
- "Currently exporting to 5 markets including..."
- "Previous CanExport project resulted in $500K in new sales"
- "We have engaged TCS in [Market] and they are supporting our market entry"

#### 3 - Strong
- Human resources demonstrate capability
- Financial resources are adequate
- Some export history or strong domestic positioning
- Product/service is market-ready
- Some engagement with TCS or partners

**Evidence:**
- Relevant team members identified
- Stable or growing revenue
- At least some international sales or clear domestic success
- Realistic assessment of capacity

#### 2 - Adequate
- Human resources are identified but capacity unclear
- Financial resources are stated but not detailed
- Limited export history
- Product/service readiness is asserted but not proven
- Limited or no TCS engagement

**Red Flags:**
- Team lacks relevant experience
- Financial instability or declining revenues
- No export history with weak domestic performance
- Product/service still in development
- No mention of TCS or government partners
- Vendors not at arm's length (contractors listed on LinkedIn/website)

#### 1 - Weak
- Human resources appear insufficient for project
- Financial capacity is questionable
- No export history and weak domestic performance
- Product/service not market-ready
- No TCS engagement
- Previous CanExport projects had poor performance (if applicable)

**Disqualifying Issues:**
- Company doesn't meet size requirements (1-500 employees, $100K-$100M revenue)
- Trading house/distributor/export broker (ineligible)
- Vendors not at arm's length (affiliated with company)
- Previous CanExport non-compliance

## CRITERION E: Thematic Priorities

**Weight:** Bonus Consideration (Can boost borderline applications)
**Key Question:** Does the project align with Canada's export priorities?

### Scoring Guide

#### 4 - Excellent (Multiple Priority Alignments)
- **Strong alignment with Export Diversification Strategy**
  - Clearly targeting non-US markets OR
  - Diversifying away from heavy reliance on single market
  - Explicit mention of diversification goals
- **Target markets have Free Trade Agreements**
  - CUSMA (Canada-US-Mexico)
  - CPTPP (Asia-Pacific)
  - CETA (Canada-EU)
  - Other bilateral FTAs
  - FTA advantages explicitly mentioned in market rationale
- **Underrepresented group ownership/leadership**
  - Woman-owned/operated/controlled
  - Indigenous-owned/operated/controlled
  - Youth-owned/operated/controlled (under 39)
  - LGBTQ2+-owned/operated/controlled
  - Visible minority-owned/operated/controlled
  - **Clearly stated in application demographics section**

**Evidence:**
- "This project supports export diversification by entering EU markets, reducing our reliance on US sales"
- "Target markets UK and Germany both covered by CETA"
- "Our company is woman-owned and operated (51%+ ownership)"

#### 3 - Strong (Some Priority Alignments)
- Alignment with Export Diversification Strategy present
- At least one target market has FTA
- OR: Underrepresented group ownership confirmed

#### 2 - Adequate (Minimal Priority Alignment)
- Weak alignment with diversification (e.g., only targeting US)
- No FTA markets OR FTA not mentioned as advantage
- No underrepresented group ownership

#### 1 - None (No Priority Alignment)
- No alignment with Export Diversification Strategy
- No FTA markets
- No underrepresented group ownership
- May actually conflict with priorities (e.g., consolidating in single market)

## CRITICAL RED FLAGS CHECKLIST

If ANY of the following are present, the application may be **disqualified or rejected**:

### Eligibility Issues
- [ ] Company not incorporated/LLP/Co-op
- [ ] Company size outside 1-500 employees
- [ ] Revenue outside $100K-$100M range
- [ ] Trading house/distributor/export broker
- [ ] Target market has existing company office

### Target Market Issues
- [ ] Markets don't meet "newness" criteria (>$100K sales OR >10% of international sales)
- [ ] Multiple markets each >10% of total revenue
- [ ] USA mixed with international markets (mutually exclusive)

### Budget & Activity Issues
- [ ] Ineligible expenses included:
  - [ ] Monthly retainers
  - [ ] Digital ads / SEO
  - [ ] Website creation/upgrades
  - [ ] Blogs / Content creation (operational)
  - [ ] Influencer fees
  - [ ] Cold calling (should be "lead generation")
  - [ ] Staff salaries
- [ ] Project budget outside $20K-$100K range
- [ ] Consultant expenses exceed $66,667
- [ ] Activities cannot be independently verified
- [ ] Costs not in CAD

### Arm's Length Issues
- [ ] Vendors affiliated with company (on LinkedIn/website)
- [ ] Consultants previously employed by company
- [ ] Board members listed as vendors
- [ ] In-market representatives acting as employees

### Previous CanExport Issues (if applicable)
- [ ] Previous non-compliance
- [ ] Failed to claim majority of awarded funds
- [ ] Breach of terms and conditions
- [ ] Identical project to previous with no explanation

### Documentation Issues
- [ ] Missing GST/HST returns or T2SCH125
- [ ] Missing Articles of Incorporation
- [ ] Incomplete application sections
- [ ] Information unchanged from previous submission
