## Company Information/Account Profile

**Describe your products or services** *character limit: 2000*

**Paragraph 1: (~700 characters)**
- Mission statement: "[Company name] is an X whose goal is Y."

- Who do they work with generally? Who is their central client base? What types of projects? (~300 characters)

- Where is the company headquartered, and where was it founded? In which geographic markets does it currently sell?

## Project Summary

*character limit: 4000*

**NOTE: The Project Summary now absorbs much of what was previously in the Business Case section, including international experience, project rationale, and activity overview.**

### Suggested layout and length:

**Paragraph 1: Company Introduction & International Experience (~1200 characters)**
- Reiterate who the company is, their mission statement, and what product/service they bring to market.
- When was the company founded, and where are they based? Have they been selling the same product/service since inception? How have they diversified or adapted over time?
- **What experience does the company have in international market expansion? What level of establishment?**
- **Have they conducted a CanExport before?**
  - If yes, how does their previous project prime them for market entrance in this one? What have they learned? Why are they entering a new market from the last or why are they returning to the same?
  - If not, what other international experience do they have that sets them up for international expansion?
- What is their dedication to improvement? How are they trying to help their clients and move the industry forward?
- Discussion of how they are continuing to improve themselves and make their product/service better or more efficient.
- Note any recent awards received.

**Paragraph 2: Market Readiness & Strategic Rationale (~1300 characters)**
- Discussion of reputation in the industry to this point, their current position and level of stability in the Canadian market.
- How has the company driven success thus far in Canada? What are the market and industry factors that have catalyzed this success? Have there been any challenges or changes in the market that they have had to overcome to secure their position?
- **Discuss how current growth and earnings primes the company for market expansion.**
- **Any initial successes in the target market that lead them to believe they can have further success?**
- **Discuss the rationale for entering the target market? How is this project advancing the company's "international development strategy" and how will it help them on their "growth trajectory"**
- How is the company primed to expand into a new market? What are the key factors that are helping them to grow? What combination of market factors and company innovation? Do they have any information on a new market that gives them a competitive advantage?
- **Why will they be successful in the target market?**
- Market entrance statement: "These factors have primed [company name] for a comprehensive expansion market entrance to [target market]. In this CanExport project, [company name] will aim to grow our business in this international market by [basic statement of project activities]."

**Paragraph 3: Project Timeline & Activities Overview (~800-1000 characters)**
- "The project presented in this application is scheduled to take place from [DATE] to [DATE]. During this time period, we aim to X, X, and X." (generate high-potential leads, gain key knowledge of the competitive landscape of the market, and ultimately, generate sales in the target region.)
- **Discuss the overall objectives in the project.** (establish connections, cultivate leads, make sales)
- **"Through this project, company employees will engage in region-specific activities in pursuit of these goals. Each activity that we outline below is incremental and strategic in scope, serving the overarching aims of our international development strategy."**
- Discussion of plans to advance the reputation of the business in the target market and how knowledge gained will increase success in the area.
- Briefly discuss the activities planned to meet the project goals, what they include and how they will help to establish business in the target market.

**Paragraph 4: Alignment with GAC Goals (~500 characters)**
- "This project is aligned with the international trade goals set forth by Global Affairs Canada insofar as it seeks to X."
- How will the project advance and strengthen the position of Canada's industry on the international stage?
- How will the project work to grow awareness or knowledge of the industry in an international context?
- Any environmental benefits? UN Sustainable Development Goals?

- Brief company description/introduction (how long they have been in business)

- **International experience and previous CanExport history**

**Paragraph 2: Financial Resources (~500 characters)**
- Discuss financial resources (or they can also be embedded in one of the other paragraphs above). There is a 50% matching contribution required by the program so the company usually uses retained earnings, loans, investor investments, or company savings to pay for the portion of their contribution. If not mentioned in the recording, assume retained earnings.

**Paragraph 3: Human Resources (~500-1000 characters)**
- Example: "In this project, we will utilize both internal team members and external contributors. We recognize that payment of staff wages is ineligible under the CanExport program. Likewise, we also recognize the need for all vendors and consultants to be at arms length from the company. For the payment of external consultants, we are prepared to fulfill our 50% matching contribution required by the program."
- **Demonstrate how your company plans to undertake and complete the project** (Business Case language)
- Summarize the Internal Contributors (employees) linked to the project. Offer a description of the structure of the company, which levels will be participating in the project and how each role will contribute to the project overall
- Summarize the External Contributors (vendors) linked to the project. Offer a description of the types of companies that you will be working with (independent consultants etc.) where they are all based, and what sort of activities you will work with them on. Also mention how external contributors will be overseen by internal contributors.
- Mention that the project leader will regularly review the course of the project, ensuring that each activity is executed according to the proposed timeline and all awarded funds are being used as efficiently as possible.
- For companies that offer services similar to the consultants in the project (i.e. digital marketing firm hiring an SEO consultant), be sure to mention that they lack these skills and therefore require the expertise of an external party
- Ex: We selected project activities that we do not have expertise to manage in-house to better the return on investment, and kept project relevant design work internal to reduce the expense of external vendors

**Paragraph 4: Material Resources (~500-1000 characters)**
- Provide a description of current material wealth, capacity and assets of the company that be leveraged or relied on in this project. These can include but are not limited to:
  - Physical assets such as office space, brick and mortar locations, distribution centres, or any equipment that the company uses to move or store product that will be involved in distribution to the new target market.
  - Technological infrastructure including any proprietary software, bespoke platforms or digital tools used by the company, or further IT systems either built by or built for the applicant.
  - Supply chain capabilities, referring to any established supplier relationships in the target market or elsewhere adjacent. This can also include a further description of the distribution channels set up by the applicant that will be leveraged in the project.
  - Market research assets and knowledge, including any market studies completed by external consultants in preparation for the data, pain points/gaps in the market, or profiles of competitors.
  - Intellectual property assets including trademarks, patents, etc.

## Market Study

*MUST INCLUDE FOR EACH TARGET MARKET*

**Explain how, in your view, there is strong market potential, how your efforts are part of a coherent international business development strategy. Describe any opportunities, identify any local partner/client leads and outline the challenges your company may face.**

*character limit: 4000 per target market*

**NOTE: Market Study now includes strategic activity justification that was previously in Business Case - explaining how proposed activities will help succeed in the target market.**

### [Target Market 1: Market Potential, Opportunities, Challenges/Risks]

**Paragraph 1: Market Potential & Rationale (~600-800 characters)**
- Open by discussing the primary rationale for entering the target market(s).
- Mention that based on success in Canada and other international markets, the business is now primed to expand. Successes to this point have allowed the business the capacity to identify new international markets (either close or far) with high degrees of potential that can be tapped into and drive success for new business development.
- Mention any specific sites within the target market(s) that have present particular potential for business growth. Any regions or cities?
- What trade agreements did Canada sign with this target market that supports Canada's Export Diversification Strategy
- Discuss the current state of the industry that the business is in? What are the specifics with regard to the market, and how do they help to present new high potential growth for the business entering?
- **What is to be gained by going after specific areas in the target market?**
- Describe what the long term outlook is for success in the target market? If there is achieved growth in this project, how will the company continue to leverage new potential either in the target market or beyond?

**Paragraph 2: Introduction to Opportunities & Risks (~200 characters)**
- "Below we identify and outline multiple opportunities that we perceive are specific to our market expansion to [target market]. We also outline some challenges that we perceive facing once we enter the region, but we provide strategies to mitigate them"

**Paragraph 3: Opportunities (~500 characters)**
- List three opportunities for each target region. Approximately 500 characters. Consider:
  - Untapped customer segments (Access to new demographics with unmet needs)
  - First-mover advantage (Establishing market presence before competitors)
  - Product diversification (Adapting offerings for local preferences)
  - Economies of scale (Spreading fixed costs across larger sales volumes)
  - Resource access (Utilizing local talent, materials, or technology)
  - Business cycle diversification (Balancing regional economic fluctuations)
  - Knowledge acquisition (Learning new practices, technologies, or approaches)
  - Strategic partnerships (Forming alliances with local companies)
  - Supply chain optimization (Creating more efficient distribution networks)
  - Brand development (Building global recognition and reputation)

**Paragraph 4: Challenges/Risks with Mitigation (~500 characters)**
- List three risks for each target region, with mitigation strategies. Approximately 500 characters. Consider:
  - Cultural misalignment (Misunderstanding local customs, values, and preferences)
  - Regulatory complexities (Navigating unfamiliar laws, compliance, and bureaucracy)
  - Currency volatility (Exposure to exchange rate fluctuations)
  - Political instability (Changes in government, policies, or social unrest)
  - Economic uncertainty (Market downturn, inflation, or other macroeconomic challenges)
  - Intellectual property threats (Weak IP protection or enforcement)
  - Operational challenges (Managing remote operations and maintaining quality)
  - Market education costs (Investment required to build awareness and adoption)
  - Competitive response (Reaction from established local players)
  - Reputational risks (Brand damage from cultural missteps or local controversies)

**KEY!! Don't include challenges/risks if you don't have a mitigation plan. You can say that the company is working with experts to ensure risks are minimized but don't say...here's a big problem and we'll have to 'play it by ear'**

**Paragraph 5: Target Client Profile (~1000 characters)**
- "Our market entrance strategy involves a deliberate approach to foster new relationships and strengthen existing ones in the target market." OR, if B2C: "Our market entrance strategy involves a deliberate approach to build a solid consumer base on which to sink our roots into the region, expand and improve our reputation among consumers, and, ultimately, grow our business in the target market."
- Provide a basic description of the target-client base and profile for the target market.
- List any identified target leads that the company will go after in the target market. Group leads by state within each target market and list the city that they are based in if possible
  - Ex: California
    - Pacific Clinics (San Jose)
- IF B2C: Describe how gaining key information about potential clients in the target market will present the company with a competitive advantage and a better understanding of the consumer details in the target market to help hone in on clientele.
- Provide a detailed consumer profile for each target market, or, if there is only one target market provide a few. A target consumer profile should identify:
  - Demographics (gender, income level, education, occupation, location, and family status)
  - Pain points (Specific problems, challenges, or needs your product/service can solve)
  - Buying behaviour (How they make purchasing decisions, preferred purchase channels, price sensitivity, and purchasing frequency)
  - Market size and growth (Addressable market size, growth trends, and competitive landscape)
  - Communication preferences (Preferred channels, messaging style, and content types that resonate)

**Paragraph 6 (REDISTRIBUTED FROM BUSINESS CASE): How Activities Support Success (~500 characters)**
- **Explain how your proposed activities will help you succeed in your chosen target market(s)**
- Link specific project activities to market opportunities identified above
- Demonstrate strategic thinking about activity sequencing and interdependencies
- Show how activities address market-specific challenges

## Competition

**Explain how your company differentiates itself from the competition, including your company's competitive advantage and value proposition**

*MUST INCLUDE FOR EACH TARGET MARKET*
*character limit: 4000 per target market*

### [Target Market 1: Competition]

**Suggested layout and length: 3 sections**

**Paragraph 1: Industry & Market Context (~1000 characters)**
- Open with information about the position of this business's particular market within the more significant international sector. For example, "the global hottub market is the predominant industry in the hydrotherapy sector internationally." Or: "Online elearning platforms and programs are quickly becoming a major part of the broader education industry, particularly in the United States."
- If possible, provide as detailed information as possible on market size and growth. What is the value in USD? What is the compound annual growth rate? If possible also provide financials and projections for the target region. For example, how big is the market in the United States vs internationally
- Describe the growth factors of the market, how it has developed in the past half decade, and what demand or innovation is moving it forward.
- Give a brief discussion of the competitive landscape of the market in the target region. Example: "The competitive landscape is dominated by legacy firms but is now experiencing heavy growth of startups who bring to market an innovated and refined product."

**Paragraph 2: Introduction to Competitor Analysis (~250 characters)**
- "Below, we outline three businesses that we have identified as competition for our business endeavours in [target market], we then offer a full analysis of the competitive landscape and argue the strength of our position."

**Following Paragraphs: Individual Competitors**
- Regardless of the amount of target markets, always include at least 3 and up to 5 top competitors (applicant should be able to provide information on key competitors in the region, but this may require some research by writers)
- For each competitor consider:
  - What the company sells, how long they have been in business (legacy vs startup) and their size (how many employees?)
  - Growth trajectory: are they continuing to grow and accrue market share, or have they topped out? What is their geographic distribution, do they have their hands in all parts of the market?
  - What is their mission statement? What demand are they trying to fill? What is their target client profile?
  - Pricing strategies. Is their product quality a more luxury or affordable option?
  - Are they doing any innovation or R&D?
  - What is the customer experience like? Normally longtime customers or newcomers?

**Final Paragraph: Competitive Analysis**
- Open with a discussion of how the competitive landscape of the market has changed in recent years (whether it has grown or declined, whether there are many new businesses driving innovation or if it remains stagnated by the domination of a few key players)
- Talk about how this competitive landscape, whatever it is, presents an opportunity for the applicant business to capitalize on potential growth in the target market (that, for example, some demand is not being fulfilled, or that the competitive landscape is open to new additions)
- Use the points of each competitor to draw out the main themes that are represented in the market ("the competitive landscape is driven by legacy businesses like [named competitor] who have longstanding customers and high retention over years, and by new startups, such as [named competitor], who are driving the innovation of the industry forward and securing clients by offering something new."
- Place the applicant business within this competitive landscape. How are they related to the competition, how are they different? How does their entrance to the target market put them in a unique position?
- Outline the value proposition of the applicant business? How do they set themselves apart from competition? Consider:
  - Cost structure advantages
  - Intellectual property advantages (What patents, trademarks, copyrights, or trade secrets do you own that create barriers to imitation?)
  - Brand recognition strength
  - Strategic partnerships
  - Distribution channel advantages? Are there any advantages in coming from Canada?
- End with mention of how the competitive advantages will be sustainable over time—that they will be difficult for competitors to copy, substitute, or render obsolete through innovation.

### [Target Market 2: Competition]

*If applying to multiple markets:*
- Place paragraph 1 in the first market section, along with Paragraph 2 and the first competitor.
- For each subsequent market, write an introductory paragraph (~1000 characters) of the specifics of the competitive landscape of that market (US West vs US in general), then following paragraphs for each competitor (try to align competitors with target markets).
- In the final market section, do as advised above and then place the competitive analysis for the entire project in the final paragraph.
- If there are multiple markets, you should be able to speak to specific competitive advantages in each. Make mention of how all the markets relate, how they are different, and how they can each be leveraged for success through a strategic approach.

## Project Budget

**CanExport SME accepts projects valued between $20,000 to $100,000.** Only applicants who are taking part in an international trade mission for under-represented groups organized by Global Affairs Canada may submit a project of less than $20,000.

**Activities must be divided by Government of Canada fiscal year** (April 1 to March 31 of the following calendar year).

Funding provided for activities in one fiscal year **cannot** be reallocated to another fiscal year. For this reason, we recommend planning your activities carefully and submitting a realistic budget. Your ability to successfully complete the activities as outlined in your application will factor into the evaluation of any future applications.

Applicants are responsible for ensuring that the activities they propose to undertake are eligible. To consult the list of eligible and ineligible expenses, please see the Applicant's Guide.

**When entering amounts, do not add any periods, commas, spaces or cents.**

## Evaluation Criteria (Rubric)

Applications will be assessed based on the following 7 criteria:

### A. Pursuing new or expanded initiatives ("incrementality")

We assess applications based on the following:
- Represents new or expanded initiatives aligned with the company's international business development goals and goes beyond the company's core activities

**Important: Applicants who have had a previous project approved**

New applications should clearly explain how the new project builds on or makes changes to the previous project, highlighting any changes in strategy or focus. For example, the application should explain changes or not to the international business development strategy; indicate rationale for repeat markets or activities; and provide any other relevant information that may help in the evaluation of your application relative to the assessment criteria below.

Note that the program will give priority to clients who have not previously received funding through CanExport SMEs.

### B. Export business case

In assessing the likelihood of the project to achieve meaningful commercial outcomes, the program will evaluate whether:
- The objectives, activities and expected outcomes of the project are realistic
- The proposed activities and expenses are specific (such as, participation in established commercial events with fixed dates) and reasonable

### C. Market potential

The program will assess the choice of target markets by reviewing if the applicant has demonstrated in their application that they have considered the commercial potential of their product or service in the target market(s), such as via market research or gathering other information.

### D. Exporting readiness and history

We will review the applicant's:
- Capacity in terms of human and financial resources to undertake the proposed activities
- Previous export history
- Past interactions with CanExport, the TCS, and partners of Global Affairs Canada (GAC)
- Explanation of how their products or services are commercialized or ready for export

### E. Previous performance

The program will:
- Consider performance on previous CanExport projects approved for the applicant
- Expect the client to claim the majority of the funds awarded

Funds previously approved but not used without reasonable explanation may result in refusal of subsequent applications.

Failure to comply with any conditions of a previously funded project will be considered in the evaluation of a new application.

**Spotlight on export readiness:**

An export-ready business is one that has the capacity, resources and management to deliver a marketable product or service on a global scale at a competitive price.

### F. Thematic priorities

We give special consideration to:
- The proposed project's alignment with the Government of Canada's Export Diversification Strategy. The program will prioritize applications that most clearly focus on export diversification.
- Projects whose target markets are covered by bilateral or regional free trade agreements.
- Applications submitted by businesses owned or led by groups that are traditionally underrepresented in international trade such as women, Indigenous Peoples, Black Canadians, LGBTQ2+, visible minorities and young entrepreneurs.

## Key Reminders

### Ineligible Terms - Avoid Using:

The program has been increasingly sensitive about the following terms used for activities, so make sure that the client is aware of the following and it is not included in their budget:

- **'Monthly Retainer Fees'** should be project based fees. It's best to avoid using the word 'Monthly' entirely.
- **'Blogs' or 'Content Creation'** are seen as operational and used to be eligible, no longer.
  - 'Content Creation' can also refer to graphic content for ads, which is eligible. Invoices should list what the work was done to avoid confusion.
- **'Digital Ads'** are no longer eligible.
- **'Cold Calling'** is hit and miss with the program, and should be written as 'Lead Generation' to be safe. Not to hire a sales rep, more to generate interest and to help applicant book an initial call with their sales team.
- **'Consultants'** - activities routed to Category G must be strictly linked to export market research, identification of key contacts, facilitation of B2B meetings and matchmaking, feasibility studies. Any activities outside of this WILL NOT be accepted. Engaging a consultant to meet and conduct presentations with local contacts is also not accepted.
- **'Advisory'** - for legal, accounting, sales, business, belongs in Category F
- **'Website Creation'** is an ineligible expense, so don't include words like website upgrades or modification. Rather, things like 'Landing Page' creation to drive traffic to specific sections of the website is fine.
- The term **'Contractor'** should be avoided and replaced by 'Consultant'.
- **'Copywriting'** is a hit and miss with the program, so it's best to use words like 'Copy for SEO' instead.
- **'Influencer'** - expenses related to influence, including fees for influencers and key opinion leaders, are ineligible

### Other Important Notes:

- **Lead generation** needs to be identification only, not speaking to the clients (even though small meetings happen regardless)
- **All consultants and contractors** must not be internally affiliated with your organization in any way, and all transactions/activities must be completed at arm's distance. They must have a specific expertise not otherwise available within your company and cannot be an in-market representative or employee that is conducting business on your behalf.
- **All consultants must be based in Canada or one of the target markets** unless otherwise justified. Additionally, if costs exceed $10K, a statement of work that specifically indicates the target markets must be obtained from the program.
- **SEO and all online advertising are now ineligible for CanExport.** (Some necessary advertorials for trade events can be accepted.)
