# CanExport Application Preparedness Rubric

**Purpose:** This rubric assesses whether a company is ready to proceed with a CanExport application. Use this tool during the initial client assessment phase to determine: (1) if the company qualifies for CanExport, (2) how much preparation work is needed before writing can begin, and (3) the likelihood of application success.

**Assessment Sources:**
- Budget Template completion
- Readiness Assessment responses
- Interview Question responses (Parts A & B)

**Readiness Levels:**
- 🟢 **Ready to Write (Green)** - Can proceed to application drafting immediately
- 🟡 **Ready with Prep Work (Yellow)** - Viable candidate but needs specific preparation
- 🔴 **Not Ready (Red)** - Significant gaps, extensive prep work required
- ⛔ **Not a Fit (Disqualified)** - Does not meet program eligibility requirements
