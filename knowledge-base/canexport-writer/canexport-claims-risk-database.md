## How to Use This Database

**For Stage 1 (Readiness Review)**:
- Review Budget, RA, and Interview responses against this database
- Flag any activities matching risk patterns

- Assess claims risk level for overall project

- 🟡 **Medium Risk**: Sometimes rejected, needs careful positioning
- 🟢 **Low Risk**: Generally approved with standard documentation

## RISK CATEGORY: Trade Shows & Events

### ACTIVITY: Popup Events Adjacent to Trade Shows
**Risk Level**: 🔴 High
**Common Rejection Reason**: "Activity not directly part of approved trade show participation"
**Why This Happens**: GAC interprets "trade show" strictly as booth/registration fees. Adjacent activities (popup stores, networking events in nearby venues) are seen as separate marketing activities.
**Strategic Framing**:
- Position as "extended trade show networking hub"
- Tie directly to official event timeline and attendee base
- Emphasize buyer meetings scheduled during popup hours
- Frame as "overflow meeting space" for qualified leads from booth
**Supporting Documentation Needed**:
- Letter from trade show organizer acknowledging presence
- Meeting schedule with qualified international leads
- Floor plan showing proximity to official venue
- Evidence of attendee overlap (registration lists, invitation targeting trade show participants)
**Claims Stage Watch-outs**:
- GAC may request proof attendees were trade show participants
- May challenge if popup occurred before/after official trade show dates
- May reject if popup location more than 1km from venue
**Examples**:
- ❌ **Failed**: Popup in hotel lobby 2 blocks from trade show, 3 days after event ended
- ✅ **Worked**: "Buyer meeting suite" in convention center, during official trade show days, with documented 47 meetings with trade show attendees

## GENERAL PATTERNS & RED FLAGS

### Pattern: Activities Started Before Approval Date
**Watch Out For**: Any activities that occurred before funding approval
**Why Rejected**: CanExport explicitly excludes pre-approval activities
**Prevention**: Ensure all dates in budget/RA are AFTER expected approval date

### Pattern: Domestic Market Activities
**Watch Out For**: Canadian customers, domestic conferences, Canada-based consultants
**Why Rejected**: Program funds international expansion only
**Prevention**: Ensure all activities explicitly target NEW export markets

### Pattern: General Operations vs. Export-Specific
**Watch Out For**: Equipment, salaries, office rent described as "for export"
**Why Rejected**: Must be export-specific, not general business operations
**Prevention**: Activities must be impossible to do without export market entry

### Pattern: Vague Activity Descriptions
**Watch Out For**: Generic descriptions without specifics (dates, vendors, target markets)
**Why Rejected**: GAC cannot verify export focus
**Prevention**: Require specific details in all budget line items
