🧪 Moderate Tier Quality Evaluation

📋 Evaluation Instructions

Goal: Determine if Haiku+thinking can match Sonnet quality for moderate-complexity Oracle queries.

This is a blind test. Response A and Response B are randomly assigned - one is Haiku, one is Sonnet. You won't know which is which until you save your results.

Key Question (Actionability):
"Would you trust this response enough to act on it without checking?"

  • 5 - Highly Actionable: Complete, accurate, I'd act on this immediately
  • 4 - Mostly Actionable: Good quality, might double-check one thing
  • 3 - Somewhat Actionable: Decent but I'd need to verify key points
  • 2 - Barely Actionable: Incomplete or concerning gaps, significant checking needed
  • 1 - Not Actionable: Wrong approach, missing critical info, can't trust this

What to look for:

  • Did it identify the right information sources?
  • Are the tool calls appropriate and complete?
  • Does the approach make sense for this query?
  • Would the output (if tools were executed) be useful?

⚠️ Watch for shallow reasoning:

For boundary-case queries (research + judgment), a response might answer the question but with shallow reasoning. Score based on "is the reasoning sound enough to trust" not just "did it answer the question."

Example:
❌ Shallow: "Yes, good fit for IRAP because they're in tech and have 20 employees"
✅ Sound: "Needs verification: IRAP requires Canadian-controlled, 2+ employees (✓), eligible R&D activities (need to confirm), experimental development approach (need project details)"
