## Program purpose

Defend Canadian jobs, industries, and supply chains from trade and tariff
disruptions.

**Objectives:** boost productivity; reduce costs; build more resilient supply
chains; reach new markets and enhance domestic trade.

**Assessment note:** PacifiCan officers adjudicate. Projects involving
technological innovation may get a technical assessment by NRC-IRAP or an
external party. Reviewers have strong financial and technical backgrounds —
writing must be evidence-based and specific.

## Three streams

| Stream | Type | Cap | PacifiCan share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liquidity Assistance | Non-repayable | Up to $2M, up to 12 months, ending no later than 2028-03-31 | Up to 50% of eligible costs |
| Pivot Project (non-repayable) | Non-repayable | Up to $1M, for projects with local or regional economic benefits | Up to 50% of eligible costs |
| Pivot Project (repayable) | Interest-free loan | Over $1M, for larger-scale transformative projects | Up to 75% of eligible costs |

**Confirmed** on PacifiCan's Types of RTRI support page (read 2026-09-17): the
caps, shares and durations above; all pivot projects completed by no later than
2029-03-31; businesses may apply for liquidity, pivot, or both; max $3M combined
non-repayable ($2M liquidity + $1M pivot); max $20M total RTRI funding when
repayable support is included.

> The Granted RA's stream table has the repayable/non-repayable labels swapped in
> its Type column. The table above is correct: over $1M is the repayable loan.

**Cost share — pivot:** the rest of eligible costs comes from the applicant and
other confirmed sources — at least 50% for non-repayable, at least 25% for
repayable. The application form also states non-government funding must be at
least 10% of project costs.

**Cost share — liquidity:** no matching funds required (RTRI RA BC). The request
is sized after accounting for forecasted revenue, available financing, working
capital, and other government assistance (Applicant Guide).

**Combining:** a client can apply for Liquidity + either Pivot stream in one
application. Max combined non-repayable: $3M. Max aggregate across all streams:
$20M per business. Pivot labour costs and liquidity payroll cannot overlap — a
cost claimed in one stream cannot be claimed in the other.

**Repayable terms:** full principal repaid within six years of project
completion; one-year grace period after completion; 60 equal monthly payments
after the grace period; interest-free, except average bank rate + 3% on late
principal payments.

## Hard eligibility — all streams

Any NO stops the process.

- Incorporated for-profit business operating in British Columbia
- No minimum employee count for BC (removed — PacifiCan webinar, Sept 15,
  2026). Confirm with the regional officer if a client is very small.
- In continuous operation at least 3 years leading up to 2025-03-21
  (businesses established after that date are considered case by case)
- Small or medium-sized enterprise with minimum $1M annual revenue, in any
  sector (confirmed on PacifiCan's Types of RTRI support page). The Granted RA
  applies the revenue test to at least one of the last two fiscal years.
- Minimum 2 complete years of externally reviewed financial statements
  (if unavailable — barrier, flag for RA team, do not auto-fail)
- **Viable prior to 2025-03-21** — confirmed by 2 years of financials showing
  pre-tariff revenue and profitability. If no or unclear: **stop. Do not proceed
  without RA team discussion.**

## Tariff impact — must select and evidence at least one

- At least 25% of revenues from exports to the U.S. or China (in the year
  leading up to March 2025, or as a 3-year average leading up to 2025-03-21)
- Directly impacted by Section 232 tariffs — steel, aluminum, copper and
  derivatives; automobiles, trucks and parts; buses; softwood timber and lumber;
  certain upholstered furniture, kitchen cabinets and vanities; certain
  semiconductors; patented pharmaceuticals and ingredients
- Directly impacted by Section 338 tariffs (Smoot-Hawley Tariff Act; announced
  2026-07-20)
- Directly impacted by Canadian tariff countermeasures
- Increase in input costs due to tariffs
- Supply chain disruption due to tariffs
- Revenue decline or loss of customers attributable to tariffs
- Other (describe)

**Every box checked must be evidenced.** Acceptable evidence: export sales
records showing reduced volumes; supplier notices showing cost increases;
customs documents; invoices or purchase orders; contracts showing lost
customers; financial records showing revenue decline; layoffs, hiring freezes,
or EI Work-Sharing uptake.

**Forestry clients:** Section 232 is the primary mechanism — confirm softwood
timber or lumber is in the product or supply chain, and have them pull supplier
notices and customs documents before the interview.

**Volume-not-revenue case:** if revenues held but volume fell (cost increases
passed to customers, filling the revenue gap without adding value or volume),
that still qualifies. Explain it explicitly.

## Sector identification

**Current list:** Aluminum · Automotive · Copper · Food Manufacturing ·
Forestry · Steel

Clients in these sectors — or whose tariff impact comes mainly through tariffs
on one of them (Applicant Guide) — **must select "Other"** in the RDA Priority
dropdown so their file is flagged and prioritized. Failure to do so may slow
processing. This is identification only — all tariff-impacted sectors may be
eligible, and all tariff-impacted businesses are encouraged to apply regardless
of sector.

**Prior to Sept 8, 2026** the prioritized list was Forestry · Metal · Machinery
and Equipment · Agriculture and Seafood. Metal, Machinery and Equipment, and
Agriculture and Seafood are no longer on it.

## Liquidity Assistance — detail

**Calculation:** 50% of average eligible monthly payroll × up to 12 months,
capped at $2M. (Total eligible payroll over the last 12 completed months ÷ 12 =
monthly average.)

If the payroll-based amount doesn't meet demonstrated need because of high
essential operating expenses, up to 50% of eligible operating costs may be
considered — **exceptional basis only**.

**Advances:** An advance of funds, rather than reimbursement, may be possible
case by case (PacifiCan webinar, Sept 15). Don't promise it; tell clients to
plan for reimbursement.

**Existing agreements:** A business with an existing non-repayable agreement
can apply for liquidity; it may be added by amendment if approved.

**Eligible payroll:** regular salaries and wages paid to employees working in BC.

**Ineligible payroll:** contractors or contracted service providers; dividends
or shareholder distributions; owner withdrawals; employee bonuses including
retention bonuses; severance; employees working outside Canada; payroll covered
by another government program (e.g. EI Work-Sharing).

**Eligible operating costs — these four only:** rent or commercial lease
payments; utilities; business insurance; property taxes.

**Ineligible operating costs:** tariffs, customs duties, export taxes or similar
government charges; raw materials, inventory, or direct production inputs;
capital purchases; debt principal repayments; dividends or shareholder
distributions; costs unrelated to ongoing operations.

## Pivot Projects — priority activities

At least one must apply:

- Market diversification (domestic and international) — new or expanded markets
  outside the USA or China
- Supply chain improvements — more resilient supply chains, optimized logistics
- Expansion of inter-provincial trade and supply chains
- Consideration and use of Canadian technology and products

**Incrementality is mandatory.** The pivot must be new capacity, new markets, or
a fundamentally changed cost structure. It cannot fund activities the business
is already doing. If unclear — flag for RA team, do not proceed to writing.

---

## Eligible project costs — all pivot streams

- Purchase of equipment or machinery directly related to or necessary for the
  project
- Incremental fees to professional and technical personnel, consultants, and
  contractors for work directly related to the project
- Incremental wages and benefits for individuals employed for the project
- Costs of meeting legal, regulatory, or government requirements
- Market development and expansion activities
- Travel costs, compliant with the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat Travel
  Directive

⚠️ Training, coaching, mentoring, and conference fees — see Open Items above.

## Ineligible project costs — all streams

Raw materials or production inputs · dividends and bonuses · acquisition of
land, buildings, or facilities · motorized vehicles · amortization or
depreciation · refinancing existing debt · hospitality and entertainment ·
goodwill, in-kind costs, or assets purchased above fair market value · ongoing
operating costs (cannot appear in a pivot budget) · anything not incremental,
reasonable, and necessary.

PacifiCan makes the final determination on eligible costs and may exclude
expenditures deemed ineligible or outside RTRI scope.

## Application form — 11 tabs

Submission tab unlocks only when all other tabs are complete.

Character limits for every field are in `APPLICATION_FIELDS`, which is the
authority for them. They are not repeated here.

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1. Organization Information | Legal name, address, CRA number, incorporation, FTE count, org summary |
| 2. Project Information | Contacts, project title, activities, economic benefits, RDA priority, start/end dates |
| 3. Funding | Project costs table (capital / non-capital), cash flows by fiscal year, funding partners, partnerships |
| 4. Benefits | Primary activity, indicator table, indicator detail, Francophone benefit |
| 5. Timelines | Full project description, milestone table, timeline comments |
| 6. Market | Project rationale and RDA involvement — optional; blank for liquidity-only |
| 7. Management | Governance, key individuals' qualifications |
| 8. Additional Details | Fiscal year end, revenues, most significant risk, tariff impact selection, tariff narrative, support type requested, liquidity questions |
| 9. Documents | All uploads |
| 10. Diversity and Inclusion | Optional; not used for eligibility |
| 11. Signing Authority | Must have authority to enter a legal agreement |

**Supplemental Form (Annex IV)** — required for businesses with commercial
projects. Canadian technologies and products; for non-repayable requests,
economic benefits and supply chain role.

**Note:** the Granted RA cites a total writeup length of 24,290 characters plus
6,000 for the supplementary form. Use the per-field limits in
`APPLICATION_FIELDS` when drafting.

### Indicators (Tab 4)

Revenue growth ($) · Export sales growth ($) · Number of jobs created (net new
FTE) · Number of jobs maintained · Number of technologies to market · Value of
Canadian content used · Value of Canadian materials used (steel, wood products,
and aluminum produced and manufactured in Canada only).

Enter "0" and a target date for any indicator with no value. Targets are
incremental results from funding start to up to one year after project
completion.

**Jobs maintained** excludes contractors, vacant positions, future hires,
employees elsewhere in Canada or outside Canada, and payroll covered by another
government program. Must not exceed the FTE count reported in Tab 1.

**Liquidity-only applicants:** focus on the accuracy of "jobs maintained";
enter "not applicable" for the full project description.

**Retired indicators:** the program no longer tracks "HQP Jobs" separately or
"cost reduction technologies."

## Required documents

**All streams:**
- Completed application form (all 11 tabs)
- Attestation form (Annex III) — **wet signature required**, scanned and
  attached; original retained
- Supplemental form (Annex IV)
- Incorporation documents
- Externally reviewed financial statements — 2 complete years, prepared by an
  external accountant
- Current interim financial statements — from most recent fiscal year end to
  present; may be internally prepared
- Evidence of tariff impact — for every box checked
- Confirmation of funding — all non-PacifiCan sources

**Not accepted as proof of funding:** forecasted revenues; accounts receivable;
commitments to raise equity; commitments to obtain future bank financing; SR&ED
credit receivables; historical financial statements.

**Accepted:** current bank statements showing cash balances or unused lines of
credit (applicant funding); signed letters on letterhead or verifiable funding
agreements (third-party funding).

**Pivot projects — additional:**
- Project plan — major activities, key milestones, timelines
- Forecasted income statements and cash flows — project duration plus 2 years,
  for current operations, the new project alone, and combined; with all rationale
  and assumptions; may include breakeven scenarios
- Key staff biographies
- Business plan or pitch deck — strongly recommended, no longer mandatory

**Liquidity — additional:**
- Payroll remittance reports — last 12 months
- Evidence of essential operating costs — last 12 months
- Monthly cash flow forecast — 12-month assistance period, with opening balance,
  monthly inflows/outflows, closing balance, available financing, and other
  confirmed funding
- Liquidity attestation — separate from the main attestation

**Optional, strengthens an application:** capitalization table; written
endorsement or review of technology; independent market assessment; evidence of
market demand (purchase orders, letters of intent, signed contracts);
productivity analysis for productivity improvement projects.

## Assessment criteria

**All streams:** eligibility · tariff impact evidenced · pre-tariff business
viability · financial capacity to co-fund and sustain operations · management
capability · alignment with PacifiCan priorities (Canadian technologies and
products under the Buy Canadian Policy — applicants must address this;
inter-provincial trade and supply chain expansion; export market
diversification).

**Pivot — additional:** project and business plan quality · articulation of
growth opportunity, not just survival · market demand evidence · clear economic
benefits for BC and Canada (productivity, market diversification, supply chain,
value for money) · technology readiness where applicable · incrementality ·
inclusive growth, prioritized — opportunities for Indigenous communities, youth,
newcomers, or rural residents, and Francophone benefit (form; Applicant Guide).

**Liquidity — additional:** demonstrated shortfall caused by tariffs, evidenced
against a pre-tariff baseline · employment commitment attested · use of funds
tied to eligible payroll and essential operating costs only.

**Grant reliance flag:** if more than 25% of the operating budget comes from
grants, flag it. PacifiCan is funding viable businesses, not grant-dependent
ones.

## Internal — Granted only

Never surface any of the following to a client.

**Fees:** upfront writing fee $5,000, payable before the draft is shared.
Business plan $3,000. Success fee on non-repayable funding varies with the
approved amount; on repayable funding it is 2.5% of the approved maximum.
Schedule B of the contract is sent once the budget is confirmed.

**Process milestones:** template provided → portal account created by client →
RA sent and returned → budget review and interview → transfer to writing
department (initial draft review, final draft) → client submits.

**Internal RA team assessment** (not client-facing): years in operation and
sector history; track record of similar projects or capital investments;
implementation and project management experience; profitability trend 2+ years
pre-tariff and current; investment or VC backing; grant reliance level.
