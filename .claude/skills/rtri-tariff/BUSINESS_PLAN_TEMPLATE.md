**RTRI Business Plan Template** 

**Purpose & How to Use This Template**

This template is designed for pivot project applications to Regional Tariff Response Initiative (RTRI). It is a **supplemental document** — it does not replace the online application form but supports and expands on it, particularly Tabs 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8\.

**Page limit:** Target **20–25 pages** (excluding financial tables and appendices). Reviewers are experienced; write tight.

**Character discipline:** Each section carries a recommended character limit. These are not form limits — they are internal quality guardrails to prevent over-writing. Hitting the limit means tightening, not adding.

**Golden rule:** Every claim must be evidenced. PacifiCan officers have strong financial and technical backgrounds. Unsupported assertions are flagged, not accepted.
