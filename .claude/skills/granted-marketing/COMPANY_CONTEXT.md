## 1. About Granted

Granted Consulting is a Canadian grant consulting firm, founded 2012 by **Stephanie Sang**. Serves small and mid-sized Canadian businesses pursuing non-dilutive government funding. 14th anniversary in July 2026.

### Business context (useful for content framing)
- Trust-heavy category — clients risk real money and time on our judgment
- Small team, limited marketing capacity — content prioritizes leverage (repurposing, batching)
- Government / grant landscape shifts fast — content must feel current

## 2. Product architecture

Three consumer-facing tiers plus a platform refresh coming in 2026.

### GetGranted — grant database / DIY tier
- **Tagline:** *Never Miss A Grant Opportunity Again*
- **Who it's for:** Consultants, associations, HR teams
- **What it does:** Up-to-date Canadian grants database, matching algorithm, real-time updates, export capability
- **Key positioning:** Self-serve discovery, no consultant required

### Granted Starter — assisted tier
- **Tagline:** *The easiest way for small businesses to apply for grant funding*
- **Who it's for:** Startups and small businesses
- **What it does:** Streamlined, step-by-step grant application tool

### GrantedPro — full-service tier
- **Tagline:** *We do the work. You get the grant funding.*
- **Who it's for:** Established businesses wanting a "fractional grant team"
- **What it does:** Full-service grant research, application writing, submission, and claims management

### GetGranted 2.0 — the 2026 platform refresh
- Merging GetGranted + Granted Starter into a unified platform
- Marketed as a facelift / upgrade, not a rebrand
- Existing users' accounts and data transfer automatically

### Granted Export — export-focused service
- Export-focused service package
- Promoted heavily during CanExport campaigns

### GrantsHub (grantshub.ca)
- Related but separate platform — grant community, resources, industry-specific information
- Complements Granted's services

## 4. Public proof points

Use these exact numbers. They're what's on the live site.

- **92% approval rate**
- **18,000+ grant applications submitted**
- **1,400+ businesses served**
- **10+ years serving SMBs**
- **Since 2012**

### When citing proof points

- Use them exactly — don't round, don't approximate
- Pair with context: *"our 92% approval rate across 18,000+ applications"* (not just "92%")
- Don't invent adjacent stats ("fastest," "most trusted," "leading" without qualification)

### Proof points to avoid without confirmation

Older assets (2023 one-pager) cite **$75M+ in funding secured** and **26 industries served**. These don't appear on the current site. Don't use them unless confirmed current.

## 6. Grant types Granted serves

Four public-facing categories. Use these framings directly when introducing grant categories:

1. **Hiring Grants** — *"Increase your bench strength, embrace a more diverse workforce, help the next generation of industry leaders."*
2. **Training Grants** — *"The business landscape changes — quickly. What worked in the past might (and likely won't) work in the future."*
3. **Market Expansion Grants** — *"The world deserves to benefit from your company. Expand your business beyond existing markets into other countries."*
4. **Research & Development Grants** — *"Canada needs more innovation and more innovation depends on new technologies, products, or techniques."*

### Business stage framework (for audience-targeting content)

Useful for blogs, lifecycle-based content, and self-diagnosis tools.

| Stage | Years | Grant fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Early Stage** | < 1 year | Limited grants. R&D (IRAP), SR&ED if incorporated. Focus on building revenue. *Granted often advises these businesses to return at the 1-year mark.* |
| **Startup** | 1–2 years | First hiring grants, first training grants for internal capability-building |
| **Growth** | 2+ years | Ideal Granted client. Hiring, training, market expansion all accessible. Year-round grant planning. |
| **Mature** | 5+ years | Ongoing hiring, training. Leadership and customized corporate training grants. |

### Internal strategic ICPs (from 2026 strategy doc)

Used for strategic planning, not usually surfaced in content:
- **Grant Amateur / Microbusiness** — 1–10 employees, rarely applies for grants
- **Resourceful Maximizer / Internal Grant Team** — 1–50 employees, has applied before
- **White Label Partners** — associations, chambers, accelerators (platform audience only)
