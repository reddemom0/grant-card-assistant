# PARTNERSHIPS

Playbook for partnership outreach — co-webinars and affiliate content with CPAs, fractional CFOs, accelerators, banks, VCs, industry associations, and tech vendors. Load when the user asks about partnership outreach, co-marketing, or partner-specific content angles.

**Prerequisites:** Load `FOUNDATIONS` and `COMPANY_CONTEXT` first.

---

Granted partners with CPAs, fractional CFOs, accelerators, banks, VCs, industry associations, and tech vendors for co-webinars and affiliate content. When the user asks for outreach copy:

## 1. Target partner types

- CPAs & accounting firms (especially mid-market, tech-focused, fractional CFO firms)
- Fractional CFOs & finance consultants
- Startup accelerators, incubators, innovation hubs, chambers of commerce, regional econ dev orgs
- Banks & credit unions
- VC firms, angel networks, investor groups
- Industry associations (manufacturing, cleantech, agri-food, SaaS, digital)
- Tech vendors & B2B service providers

## 2. Partner-specific angles

- **CPAs:** *"Grants + SR&ED: Smart Tax-Advantaged Funding Strategy"*
- **Fractional CFOs:** *"How CFOs Use Grants to Extend Runway"*
- **Accelerators:** *"Funding 101 for Founders"* / *"Investor-Ready Grant Strategies"*
- **Banks:** *"Using Grants to Strengthen Loan Applications"*
- **VCs:** *"How Founders Can Leverage Grants to Increase Valuation"*
- **Industry associations:** *"Funding Programs Specifically for [Industry]"*

## 3. Outreach template

> Hi [Name],
>
> We work with Canadian businesses to secure non-dilutive funding through grants and SR&ED. We often co-host educational webinars with partners who support scaling companies, and I'd love to explore doing one together for your audience.
>
> A few topic ideas that would be highly relevant for your clients:
> – How to Fund Growth Without Giving Up Equity
> – Grants + SR&ED: Smart Funding for Scaling Companies
> – How to Extend Runway Using Non-Dilutive Capital
>
> We handle promotion, registration, and content development, and share the leads post-event.
>
> Would you be open to a quick call to explore this?
