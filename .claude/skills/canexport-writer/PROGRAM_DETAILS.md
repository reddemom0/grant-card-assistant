# CanExport SME Program Details (2026-27 Program Year)

## Program Overview

**WHAT IT FUNDS**: International business development activities for Canadian SMEs entering or expanding into new export markets (max $50,000 per company per fiscal year, 50% cost-share).

**🆕 2026-27 APPLICATION PERIOD**: February 4 - May 29, 2026 at 12:00PM ET

**ASSESSMENT APPROACH**: Applications assessed on **rolling competitive basis** while funding remains available. Meeting eligibility ≠ guaranteed funding.

**🆕 TRADE DIVERSIFICATION FOCUS**: Greater emphasis on non-U.S. markets to support Canada's goal to **double non-U.S. exports over next decade**.

**FISCAL YEAR**: April 1 - March 31

**PROJECT DURATION**: Maximum 12 months, aligned with fiscal year. In exceptional cases, may span 2 fiscal years (e.g., pay for trade show in FY1, attend in FY2).

---

## Funding & Allocation

**Funding Amount**: $10,000 - $50,000 per project (50% cost-share)
- Company must cover 50% of total project costs
- Total project value: $20,000 - $100,000
- In-kind contributions NOT allowed (must be financial)

**Annual Limits**:
- Maximum $99,999 total CanExport funding per company per fiscal year (across ALL CanExport programs)
- Only 1 active CanExport SME project at a time per company
- Related companies (parent/subsidiaries) may apply concurrently if products/services completely distinct

**🆕 FUNDING AVAILABILITY (2026-27)**:
- **Total available**: $31 million CAD
- **U.S. market allocation**: ~$3.1 million (10%) - **higher competition**
- **Non-U.S. markets allocation**: ~$27.9 million (90%) - **strategic priority**

**🆕 PROCESSING TIMES**:
- **Non-U.S. markets**: 60 business days
- **U.S. markets (except defence)**: 90 business days
- **U.S. defence sector**: 60 business days

**Grant Writers: Strategic Advice**
- U.S. projects face 3x longer wait AND higher competition for smaller funding pool
- Non-U.S. projects aligned with trade diversification priority = better odds
- Consider advising clients on this trade-off

## Eligibility Requirements (Updated 2026-27)

### Company Requirements

**Must be**:
- Established in Canada
- For-profit
- **Incorporated legal entity, LLP, or cooperative** in Canada
- **Active CRA business number** (corporation not dissolved, amalgamated, or continued into another jurisdiction)

**🆕 Size Requirements** (Increased):
- **3-500 full-time employees** (increased from 1-500 FTE)
- **$300,000 - $100 million annual revenue** in last complete tax year (increased from $100K-$100M)

**🆕 RATIONALE**: Increased minimums ensure program supports SMEs with capacity to successfully diversify into new markets.

**🆕 Meaningful Economic Ties to Canada**:
Program reserves right to refuse applications from entities that cannot demonstrate meaningful economic ties to Canada, even if meeting eligibility criteria.

**Must clearly explain**:
- How product/service is of Canadian origin, OR
- If not Canadian origin, how export brings value to Canada (e.g., components manufactured in Canada, Canadian jobs, Canadian IP)

### Ineligible Business Types

**❌ NOT ELIGIBLE**:
- Sole proprietorships
- Limited partnerships (LP)
- Third-party representatives:
  - Agents, promoters, sales representatives, consultants
  - Trading houses and export brokers (except ag/agri-food - but sector now excluded anyway)
  - Distributors
  - Wholesalers acting as intermediaries
- Franchisees (only franchisor eligible)

**Legal Signing Authority**:
- Application must be submitted by individual with legal signing authority for the company
- Must be registered in company's online account profile
- **Cannot be a consultant**
- **Cannot be a different CRA business number** (parent can't apply for subsidiary, consultant can't apply for client)

### ❌ Agriculture & Agri-Food - NO LONGER ELIGIBLE

**EXCLUDED SECTORS** (effective 2026-27):
- Agriculture
- Agri-food (including alcohol and spirits)
- Agri-products
- Fish and seafood

Refer to Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada's sector overview. Contact: aafc.agrimarketingagri-marketing.aac@agr.gc.ca

**✅ STILL ELIGIBLE**:
- **AgTech** and **FoodTech** sectors (technology companies, not commodity producers)

**Grant Writer Action**: Screen clients early. Do NOT proceed with applications for excluded sectors.

### ⭐ Defence & Dual-Use - PRIORITIZED

**Strategic Priority**: Greater emphasis on defence sector projects, aligned with defence export promotion and Government's Defence Industrial Strategy.

**Requirements**:
- Include "defence" in Project title field
- Processing: 60 business days (including U.S. defence projects)

### ❌ Other Sector Exclusions

**Recreational Cannabis & Substance Inhalation**:
- ❌ Recreational cannabis
- ❌ Tobacco
- ❌ Vaping products
- ❌ Other substance inhalation industries
- ✅ **Medical cannabis allowed** (must demonstrate medical use + Government policy compliance)

**🆕 Unabated Fossil Fuels** (Glasgow Statement commitment):
- ❌ Extraction, production, transportation, refining, marketing of unabated crude oil, natural gas, thermal coal
- ❌ Unabated fossil fuel power generation

**Trade Disputes & Trade Remedies**:
- Sectors subject to trade disputes or remedy investigations may undergo additional assessment
- May be declined depending on risk level

## Market Selection Rules (U.S. Exclusivity - Major Change)

### ⚠️ CRITICAL RULE: U.S. OR OTHER MARKETS - NOT BOTH

**🆕 CANNOT MIX**:
- **U.S.-only projects**: May ONLY target U.S., NO other markets
- **Non-U.S. projects**: May NOT include U.S. as target market
- This rule applies regardless of number of markets selected (even if selecting just 1 market)

**Rationale**: Reinforces export diversification focus.

### Market Definition & Counting

**🆕 Sub-Market Segmentation REMOVED**:
- Brazil, China, India, USA previously subdivided → Now each = 1 market
- Eligibility assessed at country level based on sales to entire country
- Example: Any sales to ANY U.S. state = USA is existing market

**Maximum Markets**: 5 target markets per project

**What is a "Target Market"?**:
- A specific foreign country (not continent, region, or group of countries)
- Must be NEW market for the company

**What Qualifies as "New" Market?**:
Company's sales in that market in last full tax year (or last 12 months for monthly/quarterly filers) were:
- Less than $100,000, OR
- Less than 10% of total combined domestic + international sales

### Strategic Market Selection

**🆕 Priority on Export Diversification**:
- Stronger emphasis on non-U.S. markets
- Modern trade agreements give Canadian companies preferential access to nearly 2/3 of global economy
- New federal investments in trade corridors and export infrastructure support overseas market access

**Resources**: Trade Commissioner Service Step-by-Step Guide to Exporting (particularly Step 4 on market focus)

### Destination vs. Target Market

**Important Distinction**:
- **Target Market**: Where you intend to pursue business opportunities
- **Destination**: Where activity takes place (can differ from target market)

**Example**: Targeting France, attending trade show in Germany to meet French buyers
- Target Market = France
- Destination = Germany
- Must explain how Germany activity supports France market entry

### Market Restrictions

**Cannot select a market if**:
- Activities prohibited under Canadian sanctions (Special Economic Measures Act, UN Act, Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act)
- Export controls apply (Export and Import Permits Act)
- Financial crime considerations (PCMLTFA)
- Sister/affiliated company already established there (unless products/services clearly distinct)

**Resources**: Canadian sanctions - Information and guidance

## 🆕 Buy Canadian Policy

Applicants encouraged to align with Government of Canada's Buy Canadian Policy:
- Prioritize Canadian suppliers
- Prioritize Canadian content
- Prioritize Canadian-produced materials in procurement

**Goal**: Strengthen domestic industries and support Canadian workers.

## Fiscal Year & Project Periods

**Standard Projects**: Up to 12 months, aligned with Government fiscal year (April 1 - March 31)

**🆕 Multi-Year Projects** (Exceptional Cases):
May span 2 consecutive fiscal years **only when necessary** due to timing of activities.

**Example**: Pay for trade show registration in FY 2026-27, travel to and attend trade show in FY 2027-28 because event occurs in that year.

**Rules for Multi-Year**:
- Approved project period must clearly include both fiscal years
- All funded activities must occur within approved period
- Costs must be itemized by fiscal year in which payments made
- Costs cannot be claimed in different fiscal year
- Funds approved for one FY cannot be used for activities in another FY (unless that year included in approved period)

**Retroactive Costs**: NOT eligible. Program does not cover costs incurred/invoiced/paid before project submission date (never earlier than April 1 of applicable fiscal year).

---

## Stacking Limit (Other Government Funding)

**75% Maximum Total Government Assistance**:
When applicant receives funding from multiple Canadian government sources for same project activities, total cannot exceed 75% of total project costs.

**Includes funding from**:
- Federal departments and agencies
- Crown corporations
- Provincial, territorial, municipal governments

**Must Disclose**: All sources of Canadian government funding for each expenditure listed in application.

**CanExport adjusts funding** to ensure compliance with 75% stacking limit.

**Failure to disclose**: May result in application rejection, termination of active project, and recovery of CanExport SMEs funds.

---

## Grants vs. Contributions

**CanExport SMEs provides two funding types** (program determines which applicant receives):

### Contribution Funding
- Reimbursed after expenses incurred
- Applicant must:
  - Fully fund project activities upfront
  - Complete activities, get invoiced, pay before submitting claim
  - Provide detailed reporting to support reimbursement

### Grant Funding
- Provided in full per fiscal year after funding agreement signed
- Recipients:
  - Do NOT submit claims
  - Must report on results achieved

## Eligible Expenses - 8 Categories (Updated 2026-27)

### Category A: Travel for Meetings or Events with Key Contacts

**What's Eligible**:
- International airfare (roundtrip economy or premium economy from Canada)
  - May be approved for higher class if less than economy on direct flight
  - Airfare from other countries may be considered if costs lower
- Seat selection for flight
- Baggage fees (checked luggage, excess baggage)
- Ground transportation in approved destination
  - Ride-share, taxi, public transit, train
  - Personal vehicle, company vehicle, or rental
  - 🆕 **NEW**: Gas, parking, tolls for personal/company vehicle
- Travel to/from home or office to airport
- Airport parking
- **🆕 Per diem**: $600/day to cover accommodation, meals, incidentals (increased from $400)
  - Includes departure and return days
  - Maximum 30 consecutive days per trip
  - Personal days must be deducted
- Electronic travel authority costs
- Mandatory visa fees

**Participation Limits**:
- Maximum 2 travelers per trip
- Per diem capped at 90 days total per project
- Must be Canada-based employees, 18+, legally employed and on company payroll
- Travel must be roundtrip from Canada

**🆕 CLARIFIED - Training Costs**:
- ✅ **Eligible**: Training that consists of providing presentation or demonstrating products/services as part of market-entry process
- ❌ **Not Eligible**: Post-sales training or training beyond market-entry process

**NOT Eligible**:
- Travel/per diem within Canada (except to/from airport)
- Expenses in non-approved markets/destinations
- Expenses for employees already residing/based in target market
- Long-term rentals or leases
- Travel costs for non-employees (consultants, interpreters, legal counsel, foreign partners)
- Travel for core activities (post-sale training, logistics, meeting established partners, setting up legal entity, acquiring capital assets)
- Reward program points reinstatement/refund
- Cell phone charges, international roaming, data plans
- Passports or vaccinations
- Mileage for personal/company vehicle
- Premium or luxury vehicle rental
- Insurance (travel, medical, rental, car)
- Hospitality, entertainment, gifts

### Category B: Participation in Trade Events (Non-Travel)

**🆕 MUST BE IN-PERSON**: Virtual events NO LONGER eligible

**What's Eligible**:
- Event attendance (max 2 travelers per event, must have confirmed dates)
- Exhibitor participation:
  - Registration for space/booth
  - On-site booth design, assembly, installation
  - Booth rental
  - Utilities (electricity, internet)
  - Rental of business card scanners, audiovisual equipment
  - Liability insurance
- **🆕 Membership/Sponsorship**: When required for participation, limited to **lowest applicable tier/class** OR lowest tier that includes event visibility
- Shipping and handling:
  - International shipping of booth materials, printed info, demo products/prototypes
  - **Must return to Canada** (costs for items not returned will not be reimbursed)
- Business meeting room rental (dedicated enclosed space for professional meetings, equipped with tables/chairs/A/V)

**NOT Eligible**:
- Participation in trade events in Canada
- Hosting/organizing private events or workshops
- Keynote/panel speaking invitations
- Direct product sales at events
- Event sponsorship (unless requirement for participation)
- Hospitality costs for meeting rooms
- Items that can be re-used or repurposed

### Category C: Adaptation of Marketing Tools for Target Markets

**🆕 SCOPE NARROWED - Website Adaptation**:
- **New definition**: Engaging independent contractor to **translate existing or new website** for use in target markets
- ❌ **No longer eligible**: Creating landing pages, adapting website design

**What's Eligible**:
- Creation or translation of promotional materials required for target market:
  - Brochures, pamphlets, flyers, banners, posters, postcards
  - Content writing, copywriting
- Website translation (as defined above)
- Trade show promotional materials (must relate to trade show or stakeholder meetings):
  - Booth graphics, banners, posters
  - QR-coded items
  - Promotional video

**Requirements**:
- Must be carried out by independent contractor
- No affiliation with applicant company
- Arm's-length transactions at fair market value

**NOT Eligible**:
- Advertising (online, social media, print, radio, catalogs)
- Creating/adapting corporate or training videos
- In-house activities (employee salaries, core activities)
- Giveaway items (pens, mugs, umbrellas, tote bags, t-shirts)
- Production of samples and demos
- Lobbying, advocacy, PR, policy development, influencer fees
- Website application, maintenance, domain purchase, hosting fees
- Marketing and CRM software, advertising tools
- Photography
- Online marketplace fees (Alibaba, Amazon, TMall)
- E-commerce platform subscriptions (Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, etc.)
- SEO
- Online store development
- Stock footage, billboards
- Branding, labelling, package design
- Direct mail campaigns (cold calling, telemarketing, email, newsletters)
- Podcasts, webinars, testimonials, blogs
- A/B testing
- Crowdfunding
- Marketing collateral for in-market representative

### Category D: Interpretation Services

**What's Eligible**:
- Interpreter to facilitate in-person interactions with key contacts from target market

**Requirements**:
- Independent contractor
- Not affiliated with applicant company
- Arm's-length transactions at fair market value
- Must indicate language needed in application

### Category E: Contractual Agreements and Supplier Diversity Certification

**🆕 EXPANDED - Creation Now Eligible**:
- **Previously**: Only adaptation/translation
- **Now**: Creation, adaptation, and translation

**What's Eligible**:
- Engaging independent contractor to create, adapt, and/or translate:
  - Sales contracts
  - Distribution agreements
  - Non-disclosure agreements
  - Other legally binding contractual agreements required to penetrate target market
- Supplier Diversity Certification:
  - From recognized body
  - For companies owned by diverse backgrounds (women, Indigenous Peoples, visible minorities, 2SLGBTQI+, youth)
  - Must demonstrate certification is for use in and recognized in target market

**Requirements**:
- Independent contractor
- No affiliation with applicant
- Arm's-length at fair market value

**NOT Eligible**:
- Registration/legal fees to incorporate subsidiary in target market
- Other costs related to product registration and certification

### Category F: Consultants - Business, Tax, and Legal Advice

**What's Eligible**:
- Expert advice for legal, tax, international business development matters
- Fees to accounting firm, legal firm, or business consulting firm for advice relating specifically to:
  - Legal matters for target market
  - Tax matters for target market
  - International business development matters for target market
- Expert advice on regulatory issues:
  - Market access
  - Certifications for target market
  - Compliance with rules of origin

**Requirements**:
- Activities **limited exclusively to advice**
- Must relate to international business development, tax, legal, regulatory issues

**NOT Eligible**:
- Implementing or managing digital/e-commerce campaigns
- Activities beyond advice (e.g., preparing/filing tax, legal, regulatory documents)
- Consultant serving as in-market representative, acting as extension of applicant company, executing strategies, managing on-ground activities

### Category G: Consultants - Market Research, Feasibility Studies, Identification of Key Contacts, B2B Facilitation

**What's Eligible**:
- Export market research (including market-entry strategies)
- Feasibility studies for target market
- **🆕 DEFINED - Identification of key contacts**:
  - Work of lead generator who focuses on generating qualified commercial leads
  - Identifying potential interested companies
  - Initiating first points of contact
  - Preparing relevant contact lists and company profiles
- **🆕 DEFINED - B2B meeting programs and matchmaking**:
  - Organizing and facilitating B2B meetings
  - Coordinating logistics
  - Scheduling engagements
  - Preparing participants
  - Remaining independent from organization
  - Providing short-term strategic support without representing company in target market
- Purchase of information resources relevant to international business development (with appropriate justification):
  - Prepared research studies or market evaluations
  - Contact lists from reputable sources
  - Market research software or subscription for duration of project (must not include CRM or marketing component)

**NOT Eligible**:
- **🆕 DEFINED - In-market representative**: Consultant serving as in-market rep, acting as extension of applicant organization, executing strategies, managing on-the-ground activities in target market
- Contracts/mandates paid monthly, including ongoing or operational activities
- Market research software with CRM or marketing component

### Category H: Intellectual Property (IP) Protection

**What's Eligible**:
- Filing patent or industrial design application
- Application for registration of trademark or copyright
- IP professional services from consultant/legal firm:
  - Development of international IP strategy
  - Database search
  - Drafting and/or filing formal IP documentation

**NOT Eligible**:
- Application fees for certification/protection of IP within Canada
- Product development, production, distribution expenses
- Maintenance and renewal fees for IP protection
- Expert/legal advice related to IP disputes
- Development of Canadian IP strategy
- Application for insurance of IP

## Additional Guidance: Consultants and Contractors (Categories C, D, E, F, G, H)

### Eligibility Requirements for Consultants/Contractors

**Must be**:
- Independent and unaffiliated with your company
- All transactions at arm's-length and reasonable value
- Have specific expertise not available within your company
- Cannot be in-market representative or employee conducting business on your behalf
- Based in Canada OR a target market (with sufficient justification, may be in other foreign market)

**Expenses ineligible if**:
- Indication of employer-employee relationship
- Indication of affiliation

### Required Documentation

- Contract between both parties containing scope of work and cost estimate
- Work must be performed during approved project timeline
- Must clearly demonstrate work conducted for target market during project period
- Program reserves right to request evidence of work performed (contracts, scope of work, results)

**Important**: Products/services funded must be used strictly for export development of recipient's target markets. Recipients cannot profit from resale of products/services funded by CanExport.

### Consultant Expenses NOT Eligible

- ❌ Travel or per diem costs for consultants/contractors (must be deducted from claim)
- ❌ Retainer fees, monthly installments, advance fees (only services rendered)
- ❌ Commission as compensation (payment must be based on time worked, not results)
- ❌ Consultants hired via freelance platforms

---

## Other Ineligible Costs (All Categories)

**CanExport SMEs will NOT fund**:
- Ongoing, operational activities or core business activities not specific to export opportunities in new target market
- Preparing CanExport SMEs application or submitting claims
- Capital costs and office supplies (computers, equipment, A/V, office space, supplies, business cards)
- Acquiring capital assets (including real estate)
- Overhead expenses (warehousing, long-term legal services, utilities, photocopying)
- Employee salaries, labour, wages, commissions
- Costs that subsidize product selling price
- Activities to attract investment or related to development/production/distribution of new products/services
- GST (including GST share of HST)
- Refundable portions of taxes, duties (e.g., carbon taxes/credits)
- Expenses outside project phase
- Expenses for non-specified activities ("To Be Confirmed")
- Registration/legal fees to incorporate subsidiary in target market
- Honoraria for professional services
- Training costs (except as specified in Category A)
- Developing/building prototypes for target markets

## Payment & Documentation Requirements

**Payment Method**:
- Must use corporate or business bank account or credit card
- If personal credit card used for travel expenses, company must reimburse employee using corporate/business account

**Who Must Pay**:
- Applicant company (identified by CRA business number) must:
  - Undertake all project activities
  - Be invoiced for activities
  - Pay for activities
- CanExport SMEs will NOT reimburse expenses paid by any other entity

**Exclusions from Costs**:
- Refundable GST, taxes, duties must be removed
- Tips and items paid with reward points must be removed

---

## Linking Rule

**CRITICAL**: Every activity must be linked to specific new and eligible target market.

Program reserves right to request evidence supporting costs incurred/paid, including work performed by consultants.

**Risk Disclaimer**: Activities undertaken and costs incurred before project approval are at applicant's own risk. CanExport SMEs accepts no responsibility for expenses if application refused.

**When Uncertain**: Contact canexportsmes@international.gc.ca before undertaking activity and incurring expense.

---

## Funding Determination

Funding determined based on estimated costs in application. Applicants should provide reasonable cost estimates.

**Program reserves right to**:
- Approve funding at amount lower than requested
- Adjust funding for activities, target markets, or eligible amounts (various reasons including alignment with assessment criteria)
- Refuse or adjust applications with cost estimates that are:
  - Demonstrably false
  - Excessive
  - Unjustified
  - Unreasonable
  - Insufficiently detailed

High-cost estimates must be thoroughly justified and may affect application approval.
