# CanExport SME Program Details (2026-27 Program Year)

## Program Overview

**WHAT IT FUNDS**: International business development activities for Canadian SMEs entering or expanding into new export markets (max $50,000 per company per fiscal year, 50% cost-share).

**🆕 2026-27 APPLICATION PERIOD**: February 4, 2026 (9:00AM PST) - May 29, 2026 (9:00AM PDT) for projects beginning April 1, 2026 onward

**🆕 TRADE DIVERSIFICATION FOCUS**: This year places greater emphasis on supporting Canadian companies to diversify their trade, in support of Canada's Trade Diversification Strategy and objective of doubling non-U.S. exports over the next decade.

**ELIGIBILITY SNAPSHOT** (🆕 Updated for 2026-27):
- Canadian for-profit business
- 🆕 **3-500 employees** (increased from 1 FTE minimum)
- 🆕 **Annual revenue: $300K - $100M** (increased from $200K minimum)
- Minimum 3 years in business
- Financially stable (not grant-reliant)
- Export-ready: proven domestic sales, production capacity, market research completed

**🆕 ELIGIBILITY RATIONALE**: Minimum criteria increased to ensure program supports SMEs with the capacity to successfully diversify into new markets.

**FISCAL YEAR**: April 1 - March 31

**🆕 PROCESSING TIMES**:
- **Standard (non-U.S. markets)**: 60 business days from submission
- **U.S. markets (except defence)**: 90 business days from submission
- **U.S. defence sector**: 60 business days from submission

**PROJECT SCOPE**: 12-month maximum duration, activities must occur in fiscal year of approval

**🆕 FUNDING AVAILABILITY**:
- **Total available**: $31 million for 2026-27
- **U.S. market allocation**: ~$3.1 million (approximately 10% of total)
- **Non-U.S. markets allocation**: ~$27.9 million (approximately 90% of total)

## 🆕 Critical Market Targeting Rules (2026-27)

**⚠️ MUST CHOOSE: U.S. OR OTHER INTERNATIONAL MARKETS - NOT BOTH**

Applicants may target **EITHER** the United States **OR** other international markets in a single project, but not both. This supports Canada's trade diversification objectives.

**Market Counting Rules** (🆕 Sub-market segmentation removed):
- Each country = 1 target market (maximum 5 countries per project)
- Previous sub-market distinctions for Brazil, China, India, and USA have been **REMOVED**
- Eligibility assessed at country level based on previous sales to that **entire country**
- Example: If targeting USA, all U.S. states count as one market. Previous sales to ANY U.S. state make USA an existing market.

**Strategic Consideration for Grant Writers**:
- With only ~10% of funding allocated to U.S. projects, competition for U.S.-focused applications will be significantly higher
- Non-U.S. market applications may have better approval odds due to larger funding pool and strategic priority
- Advise clients on trade-off: U.S. market potential vs. approval probability

## 🆕 Sector-Specific Guidelines (2026-27)

### ❌ Agriculture & Agri-Food Sectors - NO LONGER ELIGIBLE

**EXCLUDED SECTORS** (effective 2026-27):
- Agriculture
- Agri-food (including alcohol and spirits)
- Agri-products
- Fish and seafood

**STILL ELIGIBLE**: Ag tech and food tech sectors (technology companies serving these industries)

**Alternative Support**: Companies in excluded sectors should contact Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada:
- Email: aafc.agrimarketingagri-marketing.aac@agr.gc.ca

**Grant Writer Action**: Screen clients early for agriculture/agri-food sector involvement. Do NOT proceed with applications for excluded sectors.

### ⭐ Defence Sector - INCREASED EMPHASIS

**STRATEGIC PRIORITY**: CanExport SMEs will place greater emphasis on supporting Canadian projects in the defence sector, aligned with defence export promotion efforts and Government's Defence Industrial Strategy.

**Requirements for defence applications**:
- Include "defence" in the Project title field
- Clearly identify defence sector involvement or defence clients

**Processing advantage**: U.S. defence sector projects processed within 60 business days (faster than standard U.S. processing of 90 days)

## 8 Expense Categories (🆕 Updated for 2026-27)

**A - Travel**: Airfare, accommodation, meals, ground transport to target markets for business development
- 🆕 **Per diem rate**: $600 per eligible traveler on approved travel (increased from $400)
- 🆕 **NEW ELIGIBLE**: Gas, parking, and tolls when using personal or company vehicle for approved travel

**B - Trade Events**: Registration fees, booth costs for trade shows/missions in target markets

**C - Marketing & Promotional Materials**: Websites, brochures, product samples, digital ads targeting international buyers

**D - Interpretation/Translation**: Professional translation of materials, interpretation services at events

**E - Contracts, Certifications, Market-Specific Standards**: Legal fees for contracts, product certifications, testing for compliance
- 🆕 **NEW ELIGIBLE**: Costs related to creation of contractual agreements

**F - Business/Tax/Legal Consultants**: Professional services for export strategy, market entry, legal/regulatory advice

**G - Market Research & Lead Generation**: Commissioned reports, consumer research, distributor searches, lead databases

**H - IP Protection**: International trademark/patent registration, IP legal fees

**🆕 SCOPE CLARIFICATIONS**: Certain eligible activities have been more clearly defined in 2026-27 guidance to provide additional clarity for applicants and reduce ambiguity during claims processing.

## ⚠️ CRITICAL INELIGIBLE EXPENSES

Application & Claims Stage Rejections - **FLAG THESE IMMEDIATELY**

### Re-usable Items (NEVER ELIGIBLE - only rentals):
- ❌ Booth purchases (only rentals eligible)
- ❌ Paint, decorations, booth building materials
- ❌ Office supplies (pens, paper, folders, storage)
- ❌ Giveaways, swag, promotional items for distribution
- ❌ Hangers, storage items, organizational materials
- ❌ Masks, costumes, props for events
- ❌ Card stock purchase for printing
- ✅ **ELIGIBLE ALTERNATIVE**: Rent booth equipment, displays, furniture from trade show vendors

### Design vs. Production (DESIGN NOT ELIGIBLE):
- ❌ Graphic design services, mural/banner design
- ❌ File handling fees, digital asset preparation
- ❌ Logo design, branding work (unless export-specific)
- ✅ **ELIGIBLE ALTERNATIVE**: Only final printing/production costs (must have detailed invoice showing what was printed)

### Geographic/Vendor Restrictions:
- ❌ Vendors located in non-approved target markets (must be Canada OR approved market)
- ❌ Per diem ($600/day in 2026-27) for days spent in Canada
- ❌ Per diem for days in non-approved markets
- ❌ Canadian/domestic market advertising or activities
- ✅ **ELIGIBLE ALTERNATIVE**: Hire Canadian vendors OR vendors in approved target markets only

### Vehicle Rental & Usage:
- ❌ Vehicles rented by third party/contractor (even if invoiced to company)
- ❌ Vehicles not from recognized agencies (Budget, Rent a Car, Hertz, Enterprise) when renting
- ❌ Vehicles for third party use
- ✅ **ELIGIBLE**: Company rents directly from recognized rental agency
- 🆕 ✅ **ELIGIBLE (2026-27)**: Gas, parking, tolls when using personal or company vehicle for approved travel

### Shipping Geography:
- ❌ Products shipped internationally that don't return to Canada
- ✅ **EXCEPTION**: Brochures, pamphlets, flyers can remain in target market
- ✅ **ELIGIBLE**: All other products must return to Canada with documentation

### Printing Requirements:
- ❌ Card stock, paper materials purchase
- ❌ File handling fees
- ❌ Generic "printing services" without itemization
- ✅ **ELIGIBLE**: Detailed invoices specifying WHAT was printed (brochures, banners, business cards)

### Timeline (AUTOMATIC REJECTION):
- ❌ Any expenses before project start date
- ❌ Any expenses after project end date
- ✅ **ELIGIBLE**: All expenses must be incurred, invoiced, AND paid within project period

### Documentation (WILL BE REJECTED AT CLAIMS):
- ❌ Payment receipts without detailed invoices
- ❌ Credit card statements without itemized invoices
- ❌ Accommodation statements without boarding passes (for per diem)
- ❌ Per diem claims without guest count verification
- ✅ **REQUIRED**: Detailed invoice + proof of payment + boarding passes (for travel)

### Other Automatic Rejections:
- ❌ General operations, capital equipment
- ❌ Salaries/wages, commissions, permanent staff hiring
- ❌ Entertainment, hospitality, gifts
- ❌ Airport taxes, baggage fees (only core travel costs)
- ❌ Franchise implementation costs
- ❌ Legal dispute/litigation costs
- ❌ Amazon/retail purchases (even for trade shows)

## Key Compliance Requirements

- **New markets only**: All activities must target NEW export markets (not existing markets where company already has sales)
- **Post-approval expenses**: All expenses must be incurred AFTER approval date
- **Documentation**: All expenses require supporting documentation (invoices, receipts, contracts)
- **50% cost-share mandatory**: Company pays 50%, CanExport reimburses up to 50% to max $50K
- **Claims timing**: Claims submitted within 30 days of activity completion
- **Final claim**: Due within 30 days of project end or March 15 (whichever is earlier)
