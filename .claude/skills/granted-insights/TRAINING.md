# Granted Insights — Training Grants

Load this when the program is a Pure Training grant or a Training-dominant Hybrid.

## Reasoning scaffold (internal — do not output)

1. What should a business realistically expect from this training grant — percentage reimbursement, capped per trainee, capped per employer?
2. What kinds of training are supported in practice — soft skills, certifications, technical upskilling, leadership development? What types appear excluded?
3. Are there limits around provider type — must the trainer be on an approved list, accredited, third-party only?
4. Are exam costs, certification fees, or licensing costs eligible or excluded? This is frequently misunderstood.
5. What's the reimbursement structure — pre-approval required, claim after completion, partial advance?
6. How much effort is required to apply and claim — quotes, training plans, attendance logs, completion certificates?
7. Is this broad and usable, or narrow and conditional?
8. What employer / training plan profile is the strongest fit?

## Pay special attention to

- **"Broader than it sounds" trap.** Training grants frequently advertise generously then exclude a lot in fine print — internal training, owner training, training delivered by related parties, and online self-paced courses are common exclusions.
- **Exam and certification fees.** Many programs exclude these even when they cover the training itself. This catches clients off guard.
- **Approved provider lists.** Some programs require the trainer to be on a pre-approved list or accredited by a specific body. Off-list providers disqualify the expense.
- **Pre-approval requirements.** Spending on training before approval often disqualifies the expense. Same trap as Capital Cost.
- **Reimbursement percentage.** Many programs reimburse 50–75% — meaningful, but employers must cover the gap and front the cash.
- **Trainee eligibility.** Some programs require trainees to be existing employees on payroll for a minimum period; some exclude owner-operators or family members.
- **Documentation burden.** Attendance records, completion certificates, invoices, and proof of payment are typical. Underestimated by clients.

## Output format

```
## Granted Insights — Training Grant

**Top 3 insights**
1. [Most important strategic point]
2. [Second insight]
3. [Third insight]

**Best-fit employer / training plan**
[1–2 sentences on the employer profile and training plan that fits the program in practice, not just on paper]

**Effort level**
[Low / Moderate / High — one sentence. Training claims are typically Moderate due to documentation burden.]

**Competitiveness / usability**
[For training grants, usability is often the bigger question than competition. Is the program broad and usable, or narrow and conditional? Say so.]

**Key watchouts**
- [Watchout 1 — typically exclusions hiding in the fine print: exam fees, approved providers, pre-approval]
- [Watchout 2]
- [Watchout 3 if material]

**Expectation to set with client**
[2–3 sentences. Address whether their training plan actually fits, whether the documentation burden is worth the reimbursement, and any pre-approval timing they need to respect.]
```
