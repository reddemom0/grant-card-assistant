Grant Criteria

This program provides funding to support the diversification and growth in the Northern BC economy. To increase business competitiveness, revenue, and fuel job creation, the program encourages businesses to develop innovation or improve existing products.

Grant value: 50% of project costs up to $50,000

Turnaround time: Varies depending on project scope

Eligible Applicants:

  • For-profit organization incorporated in the Northern Development Trust Region (refer to figure 1);
  • Registered non-profit organizations engaged in industrial activities;
  • Post-secondary institutions working with a private sector partner or an applied research project with private sector implications;
  • Community contribution companies;
  • Companies in: agriculture, aquaculture, energy, forestry, high tech, industrial supply chain, manufacturing, mining, oil and gas or transportation activities related to these sectors;
  • Less than 500 employees;
  • Less than $100M in annual revenue; and
  • Must be able to contribute a minimum 10% of project budget.

Examples of Eligible Projects:

  • Innovation or improvements in harvesting and gathering, extraction or manufacturing techniques
  • Advancements in site remediation
  • Productivity improvements
  • Innovative utilization of marginal natural resources
  • Value-add sector products
  • Implementation of new technologies
  • New product manufacturing
  • Technology transfer regionally, nationally and/or internationally

Eligible Costs:

  • Capital expenditures
  • Direct third-party project labour costs, including travel
  • Innovative equipment for research trials monitored by the National Research Council, or other agencies                                       Incremental third-party project management
  • Contractor fees
  • Monetized donations (ex. donated materials) may be considered

Ineligible Costs:

  • Taxes (GST and PST);
  • Mandatory employment related costs (CPP, EI, vacation pay and WCB) and other benefits;
  • Staff wages and other operational expenses;
  • Any costs and deposits incurred prior to approval;
  • Capital expenses or other purchases that do not directly relate to modification and research; and
  • In-kind (volunteer) labour.
