Grant Criteria

This federal program offers salary subsidies to employers who hire youth who have completed a post-secondary program for positions relating to clean technology. Up to $30,000 is available for a maximum of 11 months of full-time employment in STEM*  fields. To support the ‘green economy,’ provide graduates with meaningful experience and reduce the financial risk for employers, the federal government is offering a wage subsidy to facilitate this type of hire.

Grant Value:

  • 80% up to $20,000 in wages for  job ready youth + $5,000 training subsidy;
  • 80% of wages up to $25,000 in wages for youth facing barriers + $5,000 training subsidy.

Note: youth facing barriers includes women, racialized youth, youth living with a disability, indigenous youth, youth from a rural, remote, northern or fly-in community, and 2SLGBTQI+ youth.

Turnaround Time: Notified within 2 -3weeks of submission.

Eligible Employers:

  • Be a Canadian organization/company;
  • Be one of the following organization types:
    • Small- and medium-sized enterprises (<500 employees);
    • Large corporation (500+ employees);
    • Non-profit organization;
    • Non-government organization (NGO);
    • Post-secondary institution;
    • Indigenous organization; and
    • Governments of any level or Crown corporation.
  • Be able to supervise and mentor interns;
  • The employer must have an established payroll account; and
  • Employers can apply with or without a participant.

Eligible Participants:

  • Post-secondary graduates;
  • Aged between 15-30 at the start of the internship;
  • Canadian citizens, Permanent Residents or refugees (work permits not eligible);
  • Legally entitled to work in Canada and in province relevant to the position;
  • Cannot be receiving Employment Insurance during the internship;
  • Not previous participants of a Science Horizon internship program; and
  • Not an employee of the employer prior to this internship (co-op and interns are still eligible).

Internship Details:

  • Clean technology is defined by the program as technology that includes any process, good, or service that reduces environmental impacts through:
    • Environmental protection activities that prevent, reduce, or eliminate pollution or any other degradation of the environment;
    • Resource management activities that result in the more efficient use of natural resources, thus safeguarding against their depletion; or
    • The use of goods that have been adapted to be significantly less energy or resource-intensive than the industry standard.
  • Must be in a *STEM field and be related to climate change or environmental protection through the development of clean tech, such as:
    • Green manufacturing;
    • Green services and retail;
    • Health - Technologies and practices related to health and the environment;
    • Resources conservation/management and sustainable development;
    • Sustainable energy systems;
    • Wildlife conservation;
    • Carbon and climate change mitigation;
    • Sustainable planning/urban design; and
    • Eco-tourism,
  • Must be full-time (min. 30 hours per week);
  • Placements must be between 26 to 48 weeks;
  • Placement must be complete by February 28, 2026;
  • Must include a supervision and training plan for the intern;
  • The funding cannot be used:
    • For a position receiving a Science Horizon Youth Internship grant from another delivery organization;
    • For a currently filled position;
    • To replace an employee who is on leave; and
    • For a current employee moving to a new position.
  • Priority will be given to employers that hire youth facing barriers to employment including but not limited to:
    • Women;
    • Indigenous youth;
    • Youth with disabilities; and 
    • Youth from Northern, rural, remote, or fly-in communities.

*STEM: Science, Technology, Engineering or Mathematics 

Best Practices & Forms

Eligible Best Practices:
  • This program cannot be stacked with other Provincial or Federal grants.

Process Best Practices:

  • Employers should have a business number, budget, and contact information of the supervisor ready at the time of applying;
  • Employers can apply for positions prior to having an intern;
  • Please ensure job roles are highly technical STEM positions;
  • Reimbursement occurs at mid-point (50%) and upon internship completion (50%); 
    • Midterm and final report requirements; and
  • All applications are submitted on Career Launcher's online portal. There is no option to save an application once you have started, therefore you must keep the webpage open until you are ready to submit.
